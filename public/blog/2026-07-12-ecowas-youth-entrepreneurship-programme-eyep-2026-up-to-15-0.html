ECOWAS Youth Entrepreneurship Programme (EYEP) 2026 — Up to $15,000 for West African Founders

Deadline 16 July 2026
Eligibility Young entrepreneurs who are nationals or legal residents of participating ECOWAS member states (including Nigeria) with an existing, operational business — not for idea-stage founders.
Apply now →

The ECOWAS Youth Entrepreneurship Programme (EYEP) 2026 is a regional initiative by the Economic Community of West African States, implemented through the ECOWAS Centre for Youth and Sports Development with support from AUDA-NEPAD. The programme is specifically designed for young business owners who already operate an existing enterprise and need financial and strategic support to consolidate or scale. Grant awards of up to $15,000 are on offer for the highest-performing applicants, alongside expert mentorship, technical assistance, business management training, investor network access, and regional visibility that goes far beyond the cash alone.

The pilot phase targets entrepreneurs operating in priority sectors including ICT, renewable energy, agriculture, and pharmaceuticals — four of the sectors with the highest growth potential across West Africa. The programme is not for people with ideas only; your business must already be operational. If you run a registered company, a growing startup, or even a well-managed informal enterprise in one of the covered countries, you are in scope. Nigeria is included among the seven participating ECOWAS Member States for this pilot.

The application deadline is 16 July 2026 at 23:59 GMT. Given that today is 12 July 2026, you have approximately four days to apply — treat this as urgent. The EYEP application process is straightforward; prepare your business information, registration documents, and financial summary before starting. The combination of direct grant funding, technical support, and ECOWAS network access makes this one of the strongest non-dilutive, regionally relevant opportunities currently available to Nigerian entrepreneurs.
