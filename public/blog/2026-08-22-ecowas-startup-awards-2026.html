ECOWAS Startup Awards 2026

Deadline 31 August 2026
Eligibility Startups based in an ECOWAS member state, founded or led by citizens of ECOWAS countries, operating in EdTech, FinTech, HealthTech, AgriTech, CleanTech, or Tourism
Apply now →

This is the first edition of a genuinely regional prize built to spotlight West African startups beyond the usual Lagos-Nairobi-Cape Town circuit. The top winner takes $30,000, with $20,000 and $15,000 going to the two runners-up, and none of it comes with an equity string attached.

Beyond the cash, winners get a six-month acceleration and mentorship programme, regional visibility across ECOWAS member states, and direct access to investor deal rooms, the kind of introductions that are usually gatekept behind expensive accelerator programmes.

To apply you need at least two years of operating history, a working product with evidence of traction, and the standard documentation: registration certificate, financials, business plan, pitch deck, and a one-minute video pitch. For Nigerian startups with real traction in one of the six priority sectors, this is a low-friction shot at serious non-dilutive capital before the month is out.

Deadlines and details can change; always confirm on the official page before applying. Keen Africa never charges for information about opportunities, and neither should anyone else.
