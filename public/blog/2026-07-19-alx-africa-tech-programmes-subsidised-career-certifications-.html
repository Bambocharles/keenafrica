ALX Africa is one of the continent’s most impactful tech training providers, offering structured career programmes in software engineering, data analytics, cloud computing, AI career essentials, front-end development, and virtual assistance. Applications to all ALX programmes are open throughout the year on a rolling basis — you apply, get accepted, and then choose from upcoming cohort dates. Through its partnership with the Mastercard Foundation, eligible young Nigerians can access sponsored programme placements at drastically reduced rates, with full programme access available for as little as $5 per month under the All Access model.

The ALX model is built around real-world project-based learning, peer community accountability, and leadership development through the ALX Foundations module. Completing an ALX programme earns you an industry-recognised certificate that is directly tied to employability outcomes — the programmes are designed in collaboration with employers, which means the skills you build map directly to what Nigerian and global companies are actually hiring for. ALX also runs physical hubs in Lagos and other cities, providing in-person co-working spaces for accountability and peer support.

For young Nigerians who want a structured, credible pathway into tech careers but cannot afford expensive bootcamps or international programmes, ALX is the most accessible quality option on the continent. The Mastercard Foundation sponsorship means that demonstrating financial need and meeting eligibility criteria can reduce your cost dramatically. Visit the Nigeria-specific tech programme page, review available cohort dates, and start your application today — the rolling admissions mean there is no single do-or-die deadline, but every week you wait is a cohort you miss.

Deadlines and details can change — always confirm on the official page before applying. Keen Africa never charges for information about opportunities, and neither should anyone else.
