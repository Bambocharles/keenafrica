Wema Bank Hackaholics 7.0: 'Powering Possibilities' (₦200 Million Prize Pool)

Deadline Rolling, apply immediately; exact closing date to be confirmed by Wema Bank
Eligibility African startups, founders, developers, UI/UX designers, and technology enthusiasts; no formal tech degree required, a credible solution and the ability to build or scale it is sufficient.
Apply now →

Hackaholics is Wema Bank’s annual open innovation programme, now in its 7th edition and themed ‘Powering Possibilities.’ The 2026 edition is the biggest yet, with a ₦200 million cumulative prize pool, institutional funding opportunities, and direct technical mentorship from elite professionals across banking, fintech, and tech. Launched in 2019, the programme has engaged over 50,000 participants, supported more than 10,000 startups, and developed over 100 solutions from scratch across its first six editions.

Applicants choose from three tracks: the Startup Pitch Competition for founders with existing products seeking growth; the Hackathon track where teams build technology solutions within a set timeframe; and the newly introduced Social Impact Track for innovations tackling social and community challenges. Seven priority verticals are in scope: Financial Inclusion, Healthcare, Digital Transformation, Education, Sustainability, Social Impact, and Future of Work. Following the application window, the programme runs a national tour touching 10 pitch centres across Nigeria’s six geopolitical zones, meaning participants from Akure and Ondo State have local access points.

Applications are submitted through the official Hackaholics portal at hackaholics.wemabank.com. No application fee. Beyond the cash prizes, selected innovators access mentorship from seasoned founders, investor matchmaking, business development support, and Wema Bank’s network of institutional and corporate partners. Apply early, the exact closing date has not been announced, but past editions have moved fast.
