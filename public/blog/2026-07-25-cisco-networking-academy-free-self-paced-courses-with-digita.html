Cisco Networking Academy — Free Self-Paced Courses with Digital Badges (Skills for All)

Deadline Rolling — open enrolment year-round with no deadline

Cisco’s Skills for All platform (formerly Cisco Networking Academy’s open access tier) offers completely free, self-paced courses that any young Nigerian can enrol in right now — no institution, no class code, no fees. The flagship courses as of 2026 include: Introduction to Networks (70 hours, covers IP addressing, subnetting, switching and routing — aligned with the CCNA pathway), Networking Basics (22 hours, ideal for absolute beginners), Intro to Cybersecurity (6 hours), Junior Cybersecurity Analyst Career Path (multi-module, covering threat analysis and intrusion detection), Python Essentials 1 & 2 (30 hours each), Data Analytics Essentials, and Introduction to AI. All courses award internationally recognised digital badges and Cisco Certificates of Completion upon finishing.

Cisco Networking Academy has trained learners in over 180 countries, and its certifications are respected by employers globally — including in fintech, telecoms, government agencies, and multinational corporations with Nigerian operations. For young people in Akure who want to build a credible technology CV but cannot afford to pay for courses, this platform is one of the most practical starting points available. Completion records and digital badges can be shared directly on LinkedIn and included in job applications.

Create your free account at skillsforall.com, browse the course catalogue, and enrol immediately — there is no application process and no deadline. The advanced industry certification exams (like full CCNA) have separate exam fees if you choose to pursue them, but the self-paced courses themselves and the digital badges are entirely free. Start with Networking Basics or Intro to Cybersecurity and work your way up.
