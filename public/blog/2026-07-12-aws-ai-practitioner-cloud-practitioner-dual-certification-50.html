AWS has launched a limited-time certification promotion that makes two foundational cloud and AI credentials more accessible than ever before. That is two globally recognised, employer-valued AWS certifications for the price of half of one.

The AWS Certified AI Practitioner is specifically designed for non-technical professionals — it covers AI concepts, machine learning fundamentals, generative AI, and AWS AI services, without requiring programming skills. The AWS Certified Cloud Practitioner covers core AWS services and cloud concepts at a foundational level. Together, they represent the entry-level credentials that Nigerian job-seekers, founders, and career-changers can add to their CVs immediately, signalling fluency in the two dominant technology paradigms of 2026: cloud computing and AI.

No application form, no eligibility screening — just schedule, study, and sit the exam before the window expires.
