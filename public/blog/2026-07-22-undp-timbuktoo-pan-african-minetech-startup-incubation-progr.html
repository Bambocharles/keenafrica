UNDP Timbuktoo Pan-African MineTech Startup Incubation Programme 2026

Deadline Rolling
Eligibility African startups in mining technology (MineTech) and creative technology (CreativeTech) sectors, at early to growth stage
Apply now →

UNDP’s Timbuktoo initiative is Africa’s largest startup incubation programme, designed to build Africa’s innovation ecosystem by supporting early-stage and growth-stage startups across the continent. The Pan-African MineTech and CreativeTech Launchpad tracks specifically target startups building technology solutions for the mining sector and the creative economy — two sectors with enormous, underexploited potential in Nigeria.

Selected startups receive structured incubation support including mentorship from global experts, access to UNDP’s pan-continental network of investors and partners, business development support, and direct access to funding pathways. The programme’s reach spans all 54 African countries, and Nigerian startups are among the most actively represented cohorts. Being part of Timbuktoo also provides significant credibility and visibility for fundraising.

Applications are accepted on a rolling basis, meaning you can apply at any time — but earlier applications often receive more thorough review. Visit the official Timbuktoo portal at undp.org and navigate to the Launchpad section to submit your startup profile, business model, and impact case. This is a zero-cost application with no equity required to participate in the incubation programme.

Deadlines and details can change — always confirm on the official page before applying. Keen Africa never charges for information about opportunities, and neither should anyone else.
