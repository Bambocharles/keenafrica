All On Off-Grid Energy Challenge 2026 — $200,000–$1 Million for Nigerian Clean Energy Innovators

Deadline 31 July 2026
Eligibility Majority Nigerian-owned and managed, legally registered and operating for-profit companies in Nigeria with an innovative, commercially viable off-grid or clean energy solution targeting underserved communities.
Apply now →

All On — an impact investing company backed by Shell — launched the 2026 Off-Grid Energy Challenge on July 2, 2026, targeting Nigerian entrepreneurs developing practical, scalable solutions to Nigeria’s electricity crisis. The 2026 edition prioritises productive use of energy applications, including agricultural processing, cold storage, healthcare services, small businesses, mini-grids, and solar home systems. Selected applicants receive between $200,000 and $1 million in blended funding, combining non-dilutive grants with commercial financing — a rare structure that gives founders capital without full dilution.

To qualify, applicants must be majority Nigerian-owned and managed, legally registered, operating in Nigeria, and able to demonstrate innovation, a sustainable business model, and a clear plan to serve unserved and underserved communities within a 12-to-18-month investment period. The programme is specifically designed for companies at the growth and scale stage — early-stage concept ideas without revenue or operational traction are unlikely to be competitive.

This is a consequential opportunity for Nigerian cleantech and energy entrepreneurs. Nigeria’s electricity access gap remains one of the most pressing structural challenges on the continent, and funders are actively seeking founders who have already built something that works at small scale and need capital to grow it. Applications close July 31, 2026 — which is extremely soon. If you qualify, drop everything and apply today.
