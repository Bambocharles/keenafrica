Chevening Scholarship 2027 — Fully Funded UK Master's Degree

Deadline 6 October 2026
Eligibility Nigerian citizens with a bachelor's degree, at least 2,800 hours (approx. 2 years) of post-undergraduate work experience, and a commitment to return to Nigeria for at least two years after the award.
Apply now →

Chevening covers university tuition fees, a monthly living allowance, economy-class return airfare to the UK, an arrival allowance, a homeward departure allowance, and the cost of one visa application. You choose from any eligible master’s programme at any UK university — there is no prescribed list of subjects, giving you maximum flexibility to align the award with your career goals. Since the programme launched in 1983, nearly 60,000 outstanding professionals have studied in the UK through Chevening.

The award is funded by the UK Foreign, Commonwealth and Development Office (FCDO) and is explicitly targeted at individuals who demonstrate leadership potential. You will submit four essays covering leadership and influence, networking skills, career plans, and your reasons for choosing UK study. Applications are assessed by independent reading committees before being shortlisted by British embassies and high commissions for in-person interviews. The 2027 cycle is expected to open in August 2026, with the application deadline confirmed as 6 October 2026.

Nigerians are among the most successful Chevening applicants on the continent. The key differentiator is not grades alone — it is a coherent, specific narrative about how a UK master’s fits into a credible plan to drive change back home. Start your essays now, before the portal opens, and brief two professional referees early. This is the scholarship that opens doors globally, and the preparation phase starts today.
