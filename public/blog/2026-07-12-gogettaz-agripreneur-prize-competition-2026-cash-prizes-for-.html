GoGettaz Agripreneur Prize Competition 2026 — Cash Prizes for Young African Agri-Innovators

Deadline 28 June 2026
Eligibility Young African entrepreneurs (typically aged 18–35) building innovative, impactful solutions across agriculture and food systems; open to founders across Africa including Nigerians.
Apply now →

The GoGettaz Agripreneur Prize is an annual pan-African competition that spotlights the most innovative young entrepreneurs working across the food and agribusiness sector. The competition is backed by Generation Africa — a coalition of partners including the Mastercard Foundation and the Alliance for a Green Revolution in Africa (AGRA) — and has become one of the most respected agri-entrepreneurship platforms on the continent. Finalists gain access to a powerful global stage, media visibility, and an investor network specifically oriented toward African agriculture and food systems.

Winners and finalists receive both cash prizes and, critically, sustained visibility within the African agri-investment ecosystem. This is not simply a cheque — it is entry into a global network of funders, development finance institutions, and agricultural organisations that actively deploy capital into African food systems. Previous GoGettaz winners have gone on to secure additional funding and partnerships that dwarf the competition prize itself.

Note that the listed deadline is 28 June 2026 — if this window has technically closed, verify the status immediately at the official GoGettaz website, as organisers sometimes extend rounds or open secondary submission periods. Nigerian founders building in agtech, food processing, input supply, logistics, or sustainable farming should treat this as a signature competition. Your agri-business does not need to be purely technology-driven; the competition values impact, scale, and originality across the full food value chain.

Deadlines and details can change — always confirm on the official page before applying. Keen Africa never charges for information about opportunities, and neither should anyone else.
