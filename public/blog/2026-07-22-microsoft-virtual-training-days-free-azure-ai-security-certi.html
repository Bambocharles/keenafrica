Microsoft Virtual Training Days: Free Azure, AI & Security Certification Vouchers

Deadline Rolling, new events added weekly
Eligibility Open globally to anyone with a Microsoft Learn account, including Nigerian learners; no prerequisites for fundamentals-level events
Apply now →

Microsoft Virtual Training Days are free, live online training events run by Microsoft experts across time zones worldwide. They cover specific Microsoft technologies, Azure, Microsoft 365, Dynamics 365, Power Platform, Security, and are structured to prepare attendees for Fundamentals-level certification exams. After completing the full training attendance, Microsoft sends a free exam voucher via email within 36 hours, enabling you to sit the corresponding certification exam at zero cost through Pearson VUE.

Key certifications accessible through this route include AZ-900 (Azure Fundamentals), AI-900 (Azure AI Fundamentals), DP-900 (Data Fundamentals), PL-900 (Power Platform Fundamentals), and SC-900 (Security Fundamentals). You can register for multiple different training events to collect vouchers for multiple certifications. The events are entirely online, require no travel, and run at regular intervals, meaning there is always a session available within days or weeks.

For Nigerian learners in Akure and across the country, this is the single most straightforward path to industry-recognized cloud credentials at zero cost. To get started: visit events.microsoft.com/mvtd, create or sign in with a free Microsoft Learn account, filter by technology (e.g., Azure or AI), select a training date that suits your time zone, attend the full session, and watch your inbox for your voucher. Renewing Microsoft certifications is also free annually through Microsoft Learn’s renewal assessments.
