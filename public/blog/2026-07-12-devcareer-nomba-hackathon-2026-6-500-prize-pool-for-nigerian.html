DevCareer × Nomba Hackathon 2026 — $6,500 Prize Pool for Nigerian Developers

Deadline 18 July 2026
Eligibility Nigerian developer teams (developers, designers, and product builders); fully virtual, open to participants from any part of Nigeria; no application fee.
Apply now →

The DevCareer × Nomba Hackathon 2026 is a five-week virtual competition challenging Nigerian developer teams to build production-grade payment solutions using Nomba’s API infrastructure. The total prize pool is $6,500 — $4,000 to the first-place team and $1,500 to the second-place team — paid in USD. But beyond the cash, this hackathon offers something arguably more valuable: engineering feedback sessions from Nomba’s technical team, potential feature placement in post-hackathon product showcases, and direct eligibility for the Nomba Developer Partner Programme, which creates ongoing collaboration and career opportunities in Nigeria’s fintech sector.

DevCareer is Africa’s developer talent community with over 42,000 members across the continent, focused specifically on building production-ready engineers. Nomba is one of Nigeria’s leading fintech infrastructure companies. The hackathon is deliberately focused on payment technology — not generic themes — which means your submissions engage directly with real financial infrastructure used by Nigerian businesses and consumers today. Participants work on projects that leverage Nomba’s tools to address real-world payments challenges.

The competition runs from 8 June to 18 July 2026 — the final submission deadline is 18 July. The hackathon is fully virtual, removing any geographic barrier for Akure-based developers. Registration closed on 21 June, but check the official application page and DevCareer’s channels for any late registration windows or next-cycle information. If you are a developer in fintech, this is exactly the kind of competition that converts a portfolio project into a live product with real users.
