ALX Africa, a subsidiary of African Leadership International (ALI) and backed by the Mastercard Foundation with a mission to enable 2 million young Africans into dignified work by 2030, runs intensive, industry-aligned training programmes that have placed graduates at companies including Meta, AWS, Google, Microsoft, and Visa. Core programmes include **Software Engineering** (12 months), **Data Science**, **Data Analytics**, **Cloud Computing**, **AI Career Essentials**, and **Virtual Assistant**, all designed to be directly employment-ready upon completion.

For eligible Nigerians, programme tuition costs are **fully sponsored** through ALX's partnerships. Accepted and sponsored learners pay only a nominal All Access membership fee (from $5/month) to maintain access to the ALX ecosystem, community, career support, and tech hubs, this is not a tuition fee. The curriculum is project-based, rigorous, and built around real-world deliverables; many graduates begin taking on clients or securing employment before even completing the programme.

Applications are open year-round with new cohorts launching monthly. To apply: visit alxafrica.com/programmes/, select your programme of interest, create a free admissions account, and complete the application, including a motivation statement explaining why you qualify for a sponsored place. Admission involves an assessment process, so apply early and engage genuinely with the process. ALX's network of 50,000+ alumni across Africa and beyond is itself a career asset of immense value.
