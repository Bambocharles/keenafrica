The GREAT Scholarships offer Nigerian students £10,000 off tuition fees for a wide range of one-year taught postgraduate courses starting in the 2026–27 academic year at universities across the UK. Participating institutions for Nigerian applicants include Brunel University London, Cranfield University, Imperial College London, Keele University, Nottingham Trent University, Royal Holloway University of London, UCL, University of Bath, University of Kent, University of Reading, University of Southampton, and University of Warwick — among the best research-intensive universities in the UK.

The scholarship is a partnership between the British Council and the UK government's GREAT Britain Campaign. You apply directly to your chosen university's scholarship portal after securing an offer for your chosen course. Each institution sets its own deadline independently, so check the specific institution page linked from the British Council site immediately — some deadlines may already be imminent or recently passed, while others remain open. As a GREAT Scholar, you will be expected to attend a networking event with fellow UK-based scholars and act as an ambassador for the programme.

This is a strategic partial-funding option that works best when stacked alongside personal savings, family support, or a student loan — it does not cover living costs. But £10,000 off tuition at a world-class UK institution is a material and meaningful award. Paired with Chevening preparation (or as a standalone path for those who do not yet meet Chevening's work experience requirement), the GREAT Scholarship is a credible route to a UK postgraduate qualification.
