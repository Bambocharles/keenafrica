Google is putting its AI Futures Fund to work on the continent through the Africa Applied AI Lab, a co-development program that pairs selected founders and researchers with Google engineers rather than just handing out a check. Participants get early access to DeepMind models before public release, hands-on technical mentorship, and support turning a prototype into something investors will fund, culminating in a demo day in Accra.

This is squarely aimed at people already building, not people with an idea on a slide deck; commercial traction helps but is not required if the underlying technical idea is sharp. With applications closing 31 August 2026 and a co-development period starting mid-September, there is no slack left for a late submission.
