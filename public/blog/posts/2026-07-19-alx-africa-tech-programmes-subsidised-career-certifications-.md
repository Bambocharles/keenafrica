Applications to all ALX programmes are open throughout the year on a rolling basis — you apply, get accepted, and then choose from upcoming cohort dates. The ALX model is built around real-world project-based learning, peer community accountability, and leadership development through the ALX Foundations module. Completing an ALX programme earns you an industry-recognised certificate that is directly tied to employability outcomes — the programmes are designed in collaboration with employers, which means the skills you build map directly to what Nigerian and global companies are actually hiring for. ALX also runs physical hubs in Lagos and other cities, providing in-person co-working spaces for accountability and peer support.
