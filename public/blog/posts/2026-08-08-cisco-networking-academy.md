Cisco Networking Academy is Cisco's skills-to-jobs programme, running since 1997 and now reaching over 20 million learners across 190 countries. It offers free individual courses and full career paths in networking fundamentals, cybersecurity, Python programming, and IT support, taught through a mix of self-paced online content and, where available, local academy partnerships.

For Nigerians building toward a CCNA or cybersecurity certification, the value here is that you can start immediately with zero cost and no application cycle to wait for. Cisco periodically runs additional funded scholarship tracks, including global cybersecurity scholarships that add mentoring and certification exam vouchers on top of the free coursework, worth checking for once you're enrolled.

There's no deadline and no fee: register directly at netacad.com and pick a course that matches where you want your career to go. It's a slower, self-driven path compared to a bootcamp, but it's one of the most credible, zero-cost ways to build a networking or cybersecurity foundation from anywhere in Nigeria.
