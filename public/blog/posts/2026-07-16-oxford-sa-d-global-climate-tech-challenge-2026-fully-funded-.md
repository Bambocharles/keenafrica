Organised by Saïd Business School at the University of Oxford, the Global Climate Tech Challenge invites high school student teams (ages 15–18) from any country worldwide to submit technology-driven ideas that address the climate crisis. Entries can tackle air pollution, extreme weather, food security, biodiversity loss, or water scarcity. Your submission is a 10-page PDF presentation plus a three-minute video — no prior science publication or prestigious school name required, only a bold idea and clear thinking.

All finalists receive a fully paid trip to Oxford to present at the global finals in October 2026. The winning student team receives a full scholarship to the Future Climate Innovators Summer School in 2027 — taught by Oxford's world-class academics, with all expenses covered. Every approved submission earns an Oxford Saïd certificate of participation — a meaningful credential for any Nigerian student's university applications. Last year, the competition drew 1,600+ entries from 75 countries.

For Keen Africa students and secondary school groups in Akure: this is a direct, free, and globally competitive pathway for ambitious Nigerian secondary school students to put their ideas in front of one of the world's top institutions. Teams can be from the same school, different schools, or cross-country. Entry is free with no application fees. The deadline is August 17, 2026 at 11:59 PM GMT — start building your team and developing your submission today.
