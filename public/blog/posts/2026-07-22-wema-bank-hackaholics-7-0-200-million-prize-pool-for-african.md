Wema Bank's Hackaholics 7.0, branded **'Powering Possibilities'**, is the seventh edition of Nigeria's leading bank-backed innovation programme, and it has expanded significantly in 2026 with three distinct competition tracks, a larger geographic reach across Africa, and a **₦200 million cumulative prize pool**. Applications are officially open and the programme has engaged over 50,000 participants and supported more than 10,000 startups since inception.

The three tracks accommodate builders at every stage: the **Hackathon Track** is a timed build challenge for developers, designers, and product thinkers; the **Ideathon Track** is for early-stage concepts and problem exploration; and the **Startup Pitch Competition** is for founders with existing products seeking growth capital and visibility. Priority problem domains include Financial Inclusion, Healthcare, Digital Transformation, Sustainability, Education, and the Future of Work.

Beyond prizes, Hackaholics winners access funding opportunities, direct mentorship from banking and fintech executives, strategic partnerships, investor introductions, and an accelerator programme for scaling selected solutions. There is no application fee, no programming degree required, and the competition is open to individuals and teams. Visit hackaholics.wemabank.com to apply directly.
