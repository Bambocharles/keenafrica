Now in its 11th edition, AEIC is organised by the Nigeria Energy Forum and has built a track record of pushing early-stage teams straight into rooms with investors and industry decision-makers. This year's theme, accelerating value addition, youth innovation, and community action, reflects a deliberate push toward practical, deployable ideas over pure concept pitches.

Winning teams share cash prizes of up to $10,000, and finalists, even those who do not win, get business development mentorship and incubator support valued at over $15,000. Twenty or more shortlisted teams also receive complimentary passes to the 11th Nigeria Energy Forum in November, a networking opportunity that alone is worth the application effort.

The team-composition requirement, at least one woman and one man per team, is unusual and worth planning around early if you are assembling a group. Submissions are proposals at this stage; shortlisted teams pitch prototype models virtually ahead of the November forum.
