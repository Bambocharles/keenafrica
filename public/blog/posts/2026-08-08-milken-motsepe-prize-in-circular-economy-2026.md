The Milken-Motsepe Prize, a partnership between the Milken Institute and the Motsepe Foundation, is one of the largest single prize pools available to African entrepreneurs this year: $2 million total, including a $1 million grand prize, aimed at businesses transforming how Africa manages waste and materials. Ten semifinalists each receive $50,000, and five finalists receive an additional $50,000 on top of that, so even reaching the later rounds is a meaningful capital injection.

The prize specifically wants technology-enabled, commercially viable businesses, not pilot projects or research papers, that reduce waste, keep materials in circulation longer, and create real jobs and environmental impact. This makes it a strong fit for Nigerian founders working in recycling, waste-to-value, or sustainable materials who have moved past the idea stage.

The deadline is tight, 13 August 2026, so this is one to act on immediately if it fits your venture. Applications are free and submitted directly through the official Milken-Motsepe Prize website.
