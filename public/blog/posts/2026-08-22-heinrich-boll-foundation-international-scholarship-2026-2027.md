The Heinrich Boll Foundation, affiliated with Germany's Green Party, funds graduate study for students who combine strong academics with civic or political engagement. Non-EU scholars receive a monthly stipend of 992 euros for Master's study (1,400 euros for doctoral study), a mobility allowance, health insurance support, and a 300 euro monthly book allowance for the full duration of the degree.

The application is entirely online and free: transcripts, a motivation letter connecting your academic plans to social or political engagement, and references. The Foundation places real weight on the engagement piece, so applicants who can point to concrete community or advocacy work stand out.

For Nigerians eyeing German universities, many of which charge no tuition even for international students, this scholarship removes the remaining cost barrier: living expenses. Applications for the Fall 2026 intake close 1 September 2026, with a second window opening 15 January 2027 for Spring intake.
