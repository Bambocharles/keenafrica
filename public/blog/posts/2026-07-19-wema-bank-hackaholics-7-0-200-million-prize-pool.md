Wema Bank Hackaholics 7.0 is the seventh edition of Nigeria's premier bank-backed open innovation programme, offering a cumulative ₦200 million in prizes — the largest prize pool in the programme's history. The competition is built around three tracks: Financial Inclusion (bridging banking access gaps for the unbanked), Healthcare (software solutions for medical delivery and access), and Digital Transformation (modernising traditional processes with cutting-edge infrastructure). Teams are required to design, develop, and pitch minimum viable products or prototypes that address real-world challenges within these tracks.

Beyond the cash prizes, Hackaholics offers winners direct API access to Wema Bank's proprietary banking infrastructure and sandboxes, institutional funding opportunities, elite networking with venture capitalists and banking industry executives, and direct mentorship from elite professionals. Selected projects may also become launch candidates within Wema Bank's own innovation pipeline — potentially scaling from hackathon submission to deployed product.

Hackaholics operates in a hybrid model: virtual workshops and physical regional pitch events. Teams of up to three members register on the official portal, submit a detailed description of their digital solution, the target track, and their technology stack. The ₦200 million prize pool is equity-free — you build, you pitch, and if you win, the money is yours. This is one of the most lucrative and practically valuable hackathons open in Nigeria right now.
