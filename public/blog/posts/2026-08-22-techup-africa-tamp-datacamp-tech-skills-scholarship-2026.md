TechUp Africa partners with DataCamp Donates to distribute premium DataCamp licenses, normally a paid subscription, to young Africans at no cost. The scholarship unlocks the full course catalogue: Python, SQL, Power BI, machine learning, and applied data science, along with practice exercises, skill assessments, and DataCamp's Certification and Workspace features.

The application itself is a short form covering your background and current situation, with no fee at any stage. The one condition worth noting: TechUp Africa can revoke the license if it sits unused for two consecutive months, so this is best suited to applicants who are ready to start immediately rather than banking the credential for later.

For a Nigerian building toward a data or AI career, a DataCamp license normally costs real money every month, this scholarship removes that cost entirely for the duration of active study, provided you actually put in the hours.
