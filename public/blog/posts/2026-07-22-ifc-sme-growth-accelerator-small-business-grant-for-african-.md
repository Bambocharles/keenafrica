The International Finance Corporation (IFC), the private-sector arm of the World Bank Group, runs the SME Growth Accelerator to support high-potential small and medium businesses across Africa. The grant is non-dilutive — meaning you keep full ownership of your company — and is designed for founders looking to expand operations, increase production capacity, strengthen distribution channels, or improve business infrastructure.

Applicants are expected to present a clear market opportunity, a sustainable business model, and measurable growth projections. Strong financial projections and a practical use-of-funds strategy significantly increase approval chances. The IFC brings not just funding but also its global network of technical experts, market intelligence, and investor connections — making this an unusually high-value opportunity beyond the capital itself.

The application window runs to **15 November 2026**, giving you ample time to prepare a strong submission. Visit the official IFC page, complete the online application form with your business details, financial model, and growth plan. Given the IFC's institutional weight, a well-structured application that speaks the language of development impact alongside commercial viability will stand out.
