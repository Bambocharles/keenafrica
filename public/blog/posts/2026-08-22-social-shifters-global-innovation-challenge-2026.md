Social Shifters runs this challenge specifically for young people who have moved past the idea stage: your project needs to already be operating and producing measurable outputs tied to at least one UN Sustainable Development Goal. That bar filters out idea-stage applicants, which means less competition for founders who are actually building.

Multiple awards of up to $15,000 are available, alongside connection to a global community of young impact leaders and exposure through Social Shifters' partner brands. The application runs through the organisation's own awards platform and is free to enter.

For a Nigerian founder running an early but proven venture, whether in agritech, education, health, or climate, this is real non-dilutive capital with a fast, straightforward process and a deadline that gives you time to prepare a tight submission.
