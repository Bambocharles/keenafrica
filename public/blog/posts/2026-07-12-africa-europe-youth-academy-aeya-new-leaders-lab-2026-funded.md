The Africa-Europe Youth Academy New Leaders Lab (NLL) is a leadership and entrepreneurship programme built for young Africans and Europeans committed to building cross-continental solutions. The programme combines structured leadership training, mentorship from experienced practitioners, and deliberate networking between African and European innovators, the kind of contact that builds partnerships, opens markets, and creates careers. Selected participants from Nigeria and other African countries gain access to a curated cohort of peers and mentors operating at the intersection of policy, business, and social innovation.

The New Leaders Lab places particular emphasis on entrepreneurial problem-solving and applied leadership, attendees are not passive participants but are expected to bring and develop their own initiatives during the programme. For Nigerian founders and young professionals working in tech, policy, or social enterprise, this represents a unique opportunity to access European networks and amplify the reach and credibility of their work beyond West Africa.

The application deadline is 19 July 2026. With today being 12 July, this is a seven-day window, prioritise your application immediately. The programme is funded, covering core participation costs, which makes it accessible regardless of your financial position. Applications are evaluated on leadership potential, project impact, and alignment with the Academy's goals of strengthening Africa-Europe collaboration.
