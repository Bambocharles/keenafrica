The MEST Africa Challenge is one of the continent's most established pitch competitions, run by the Meltwater Entrepreneurial School of Technology, a pan-African training and investment programme with over a decade of track record. Winners receive $50,000 in equity investment, entry into the MEST portfolio, a year of professional business coaching, and access to MEST's network of investors and corporate partners.

Unlike many pitch competitions that reward polished decks over real businesses, MEST is looking for startups with actual traction that are ready to scale into new markets, and applicants must pitch live on camera rather than submit a produced video. Two finalists are selected from each eligible country, including Nigeria, to compete at the Demo Day in Accra.

Applications close 30 August 2026, and there's no fee to apply. If your startup already has customers and revenue and you need capital plus structure to grow, this is a credible, well-resourced route to both.
