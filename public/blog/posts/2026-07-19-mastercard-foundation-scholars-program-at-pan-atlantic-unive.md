The Mastercard Foundation Scholars Program at Pan-Atlantic University provides full scholarships covering tuition, accommodation, feeding, books, and a personal development stipend to exceptional young Africans who would otherwise be unable to access high-quality university education. Pan-Atlantic University is home to Lagos Business School, one of the highest-ranked business schools in Africa — meaning scholars gain access to faculty, networks, and alumni that connect directly into Nigeria's top corporate and entrepreneurial ecosystem.

The Programme's mission is to prepare young people to be agents of change in their communities, countries, and regions. Scholars are supported not just financially but through structured mentorship, career development workshops, and a pan-African network of alumni. The focus on financial need alongside academic excellence means this scholarship prioritises those who are most ambitious but most constrained — precisely the profile of many young Nigerians building their futures against the odds.

Applications are typically reviewed on a rolling basis. Visit the official PAU/Mastercard Foundation page directly to confirm the current cohort timeline, submit your expression of interest, and complete required documentation early. This is a competitive award — a strong personal statement demonstrating leadership within your community and a clear vision for how you will use your degree to create impact is what separates shortlisted candidates from the rest.
