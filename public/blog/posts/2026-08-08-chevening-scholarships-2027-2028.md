Chevening is the UK government's global scholarship programme, funded by the Foreign, Commonwealth and Development Office. It pays for a one-year master's degree at any UK university you're admitted to, plus return flights, a monthly living stipend, and a arrival allowance. There is no restriction on field of study, which means engineers, policy wonks, and creatives compete on equal footing.

The real value is the network: Chevening builds a global community of over 60,000 alumni, many of whom now run ministries, startups, and NGOs across Africa. The application asks you to write four essays covering leadership, networking, career plans, and course choice, so it rewards applicants who already have a clear story about what they've built and where they're headed.

You'll need an unconditional offer from a UK university by around July 2027, so start your university applications now even as you work on the Chevening essays. Competition is steep, but Nigeria consistently produces one of the largest Chevening cohorts on the continent.
