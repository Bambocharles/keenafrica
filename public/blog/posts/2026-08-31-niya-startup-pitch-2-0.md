NiYA Startup Pitch 2.0 is the Nigerian Youth Academy's follow-up to its first funding cycle, and it's built specifically to find the next wave of youth-led tech founders outside the usual Lagos-only circuit. Applicants need a working team (at least two co-founders), a pitch deck, and a completed NiYA profile; selected startups get a share of the ₦100 million pool alongside structured mentorship and nationwide visibility.

Because this is a federal government initiative run on niya.gov.ng, there is no application fee and reviews happen on a rolling basis, so earlier, well-prepared submissions have an edge. If you have a working prototype and co-founders ready to go, this is a direct, no-nonsense route to capital and a national platform.
