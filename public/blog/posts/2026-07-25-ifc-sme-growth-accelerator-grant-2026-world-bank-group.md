The International Finance Corporation (IFC), a member of the World Bank Group, has launched its SME Growth Accelerator Grant for 2026. This is serious, institutional-grade funding: successful applicants can receive between USD $50,000 and USD $1.5 million in non-dilutive grant money, meaning you keep 100% equity in your business. The grant is designed to fund working capital, pilot deployments, technology procurement, market research, climate and ESG investments, and capacity-building activities.

Beyond the cash, participants receive mentorship from experienced business and industry experts, access to IFC's global investment and business networks, and opportunities for follow-on financing. The programme is specifically targeting SMEs in Africa, Asia, and Latin America that are for-profit, demonstrably viable, and capable of creating quality jobs. If your Nigerian startup or SME has crossed the revenue threshold and you are thinking about scaling into new markets or strengthening operations, this programme was designed for you.

The application process is streamlined, submissions take approximately 15 minutes online, with no application fee, and decisions are typically made within four to six weeks. The deadline is 15 November 2026. Nigerian founders in fintech, agritech, healthtech, clean energy, logistics, and manufacturing should prioritise this one, the grant sizes are among the largest available to African SMEs from a development institution.
