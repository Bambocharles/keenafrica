Launched by DevCareer, Africa's developer talent community with over 42,000 members, in partnership with Nomba (one of Nigeria's leading fintech infrastructure companies), this hackathon challenges teams to build production-grade payment integrations on Nomba's API stack. The competition is fully virtual, runs from **8 June to 18 July 2026**, and requires no travel, any Nigerian developer team can compete from Akure, Ibadan, Kano, or Lagos.

The prize pool of $6,500 is distributed across the top teams: the winning team receives $4,000, second place earns $1,500, with additional awards for other top performers. Beyond cash, top teams enter the **Nomba Developer Partner Program**, gaining access to ongoing collaboration, engineering feedback sessions, and the potential to have their solutions featured in Nomba's production roadmap. This is one of the few competitions that explicitly creates a post-competition career pathway.

The focus on payment technology is strategic, Nigeria's fintech sector is the largest in Africa, and developers who can build on real payment infrastructure have significant market value. To compete, teams work with Nomba's payment tools to address real-world business and consumer needs.
