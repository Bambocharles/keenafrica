GITEX Nigeria runs from 31 August to 3 September 2026 across Abuja and Lagos, with the main Tech Expo and Future Economy Conference happening 2 to 3 September at the Lagos leg. It is the West African edition of the GITEX franchise, the same brand behind GITEX Global in Dubai, and it pulls in government leaders, telcos, cloud providers, and startup founders under one roof to talk AI, infrastructure, and the future economy.

Organizers have made general registration completely free for this edition, which is unusual for an expo of this scale. If you can get to Lagos in the next two days, walking the floor and sitting in on the AI Summit sessions is a low-cost way to meet people building the products you will compete or partner with next.
