The Nigerian Economic Summit Group has run this annual summit for over three decades as the country's most influential private-sector-led policy dialogue. NES #32, themed 'Growth That Works: Delivering Jobs, Productivity and Shared Prosperity,' takes place 26 to 27 October 2026 at the Transcorp Hilton in Abuja, and pre-registration is open now.

This is not a startup pitch event, it is where the people who write Nigeria's economic policy sit across the table from the people affected by it. For a young Nigerian serious about influencing policy on jobs, tech regulation, or productivity rather than just reacting to it, getting a seat in the room is worth the trip to Abuja.
