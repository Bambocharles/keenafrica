timbuktoo is UNDP's flagship continental initiative to make Africa a global hub for innovation, with a stated ambition to invest $1 billion, support 10,000 startups, and scale 1,000 high-growth companies over ten years. The Launchpad is the on-ramp: a venture-building programme that identifies promising early-stage startups and prepares them for serious investment through structured incubation, mentorship, and connections into a distributed network of hubs and investors across the continent.

What makes this different from a typical accelerator is the institutional weight behind it and the sector-specific tracks, currently running open calls for GreenTech, AgriTech, EdTech, CreativeTech, and MineTech startups. Nigerian founders building in any of these verticals are eligible to apply, and UNDP runs local calls through its country offices as well as pan-African ones.

Applications are accepted on a rolling basis with startups admitted through quarterly cohorts, the next of which begins in September 2026. There's no application fee. Because tracks open and close on their own schedules, check the current call for your sector before you apply, and apply early since cohort slots are limited.
