Hackaholics 7.0 is Wema Bank's premier annual open innovation programme — and at ₦200 million in cumulative prizes, it is one of the largest hackathon prize pools in Nigeria's history. Teams are challenged to design, develop, and pitch minimum viable products (MVPs) or prototypes across four key tracks: Financial Inclusion (banking access for the unbanked), Healthcare (software for medical delivery and access), Digital Transformation (modernising traditional processes), and a general innovation track.

Winners do not just walk away with cash — they get direct API access to Wema Bank's proprietary banking infrastructure to validate and integrate their solutions in a real production environment. Top teams also gain direct pitch access to venture capitalists, tech ecosystem leaders, and corporate executives, with selected projects featured in post-hackathon product showcases. The programme is hybrid: virtual workshops are held online, while regional pitch events are held in person across Nigeria.
