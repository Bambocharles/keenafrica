Cisco's Skills for All platform hosts the same curriculum used in the CCNA pathway, starting with Introduction to Networks, alongside standalone courses in Linux, Python, IoT analytics, and cybersecurity fundamentals. Courses are self-paced and self-enroll, so there's no application to wait on, you start the day you sign up.

IEEE Nigeria also runs a parallel collaboration with Cisco offering guided cohorts through the same foundation courses, useful if you'd rather learn alongside a group than solo. Either way, note that completing a free course earns a digital badge; the paid professional-level Cisco exams are a separate, later step if you want to go further.
