Chevening is the gold standard of international scholarships. Funded by the UK Foreign, Commonwealth and Development Office (FCDO), it covers 100% of tuition fees at any eligible UK university, a monthly living allowance, economy-class return flights, an arrival allowance, and visa application costs, a total package often exceeding £30,000–£50,000. The 2027/2028 application window opens in August 2026 and closes on **6 October 2026**, giving you a narrow but real preparation window right now.

Nigeria is one of Chevening's most active eligible countries, and past scholars include doctors, engineers, journalists, policymakers, and entrepreneurs who returned to build transformative careers at home. The application asks for four 500-word essays on leadership, networking, future plans, and why you chose your courses, so the work is intellectual, not procedural. You must apply to three eligible UK universities as part of the process, though you do not need an offer before submitting.

The competition is intense: fewer than 1% of Nigerian applicants were selected in the 2024–2025 cycle. Winners are not the smartest in the room; they are the most prepared, with concrete leadership stories and a credible post-study plan tied to Nigeria's development. Start your essays, shortlist three UK programmes, and take your IELTS test now. Do not wait until August.
