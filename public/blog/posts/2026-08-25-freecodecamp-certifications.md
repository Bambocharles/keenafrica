freeCodeCamp is a nonprofit that has run entirely free, certification-backed coding curricula for over a decade, covering responsive web design, JavaScript algorithms, data analysis with Python, and machine learning, among others. Each certification requires you to build and ship real projects, not just pass quizzes, so what you walk away with is a portfolio, not just a badge.

There's no cohort to wait for and no acceptance process: you create a free account and start the curriculum immediately, working at your own pace. For anyone building toward a tech role without the budget for a bootcamp, it's one of the few free options with genuine industry recognition attached to the name.
