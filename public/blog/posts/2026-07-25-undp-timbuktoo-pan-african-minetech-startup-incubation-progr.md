The UNDP Timbuktoo Pan-African MineTech Startup Incubation Programme is part of UNDP's flagship Timbuktoo initiative, which supports African startup ecosystems across multiple verticals. The MineTech cohort is specifically targeting early-stage and growth-stage African startups using technology, including AI, IoT, data analytics, and automation, to transform mining and mineral value chains across the continent. Africa sits on an estimated 30% of the world's mineral reserves, and the intersection of technology with this sector represents one of the highest-potential opportunities for African founders.

Selected startups receive incubation support including business development training, technical mentorship from UNDP experts and industry partners, access to networks of investors and mining companies, and market linkage opportunities. The programme is open to founders from all 54 African countries, and the application process is fully online with no fees. Nigerian founders working on mine safety, mineral traceability, environmental compliance, or extraction efficiency should look closely at this opportunity.

Applications are open on a rolling basis. Visit the Timbuktoo portal to confirm the current intake window and submit your application. UNDP's reach across African governments and development institutions means that participants gain credibility and exposure that goes well beyond the incubation period itself.
