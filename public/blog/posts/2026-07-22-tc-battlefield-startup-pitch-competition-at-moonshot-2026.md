TC Battlefield is the official startup pitch competition embedded within Moonshot by TechCabal, Africa's fastest-growing tech conference. Selected finalists pitch live on the Moonshot main stage on October 28–29 in Lagos, in front of thousands of attendees including investors who have collectively deployed over $5 billion through their funds. The historical prize pool has included $15,000 (1st), $10,000 (2nd), and $5,000 (3rd place), plus media coverage across TechCabal's platform, which reaches Africa's entire tech ecosystem.

Application to TC Battlefield is **free**. Startups are evaluated on product innovation, market size, team capability, traction, and the quality of their pitch. The competition is structured to amplify African founders who are building solutions with global potential, making it an ideal platform for Nigerians working on fintech, agritech, healthtech, edtech, and climate tech.

Beyond the prize money, TC Battlefield alumni consistently report that the investor connections made at Moonshot accelerated their fundraising by months. Register your startup at moonshot.techcabal.com/battlefield/ and submit your pitch application. With October fast approaching, applications are open now.
