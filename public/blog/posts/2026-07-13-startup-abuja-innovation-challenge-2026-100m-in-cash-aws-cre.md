Organised by Startup Abuja in partnership with Transnet Cloud and Amazon Web Services (AWS), the 2026 Startup Innovation Challenge is structured as a hackathon-plus-growth-program hybrid, a national online competition that does not require founders to relocate or travel. The prize pool exceeds ₦100 million in total value. The first-place winner receives ₦5 million in cash, ₦4 million in AWS credits, and ₦1 million worth of tailored business mentorship. Second and third place packages are similarly structured, and 10 runner-ups each receive ₦200,000 cash plus ₦1.5 million in AWS credits. All selected participants also receive ₦500,000 in AWS credits to build and test their solutions on cloud infrastructure.

The program is open to Nigerians, other Africans, or non-Africans building solutions for the African market, all genders and ages are eligible. To enter, you need a functional website demonstrating your working prototype or MVP. The focus is on tech-enabled, scalable solutions that can demonstrate real-world impact. Prizes are equity-free: no shares, no dilution.

Participants also gain access to an AWS-powered development environment through Transnet Cloud during the competition, networking with investors, and visibility through post-competition showcases. For a Nigerian founder with a product already in motion, this is one of the most accessible and high-value competitions available domestically.
