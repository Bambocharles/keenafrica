Schwarzman Scholars is a highly selective, fully funded master's programme at Tsinghua University in Beijing, designed explicitly to prepare future leaders to understand China and navigate a multipolar world. The scholarship covers tuition, room and board, a laptop, travel to and from China, and an in-country study tour, so the only cost to you is your time and ambition.

The programme is modeled on the Rhodes Scholarship but built for a world where understanding China matters as much as understanding the West. Cohorts are small and intensely international, drawing students from the US, Europe, Asia, and a growing number from Africa, which means the network you build rivals the classroom itself.

The US and Global application for this cycle is open from April 2026 and closes 9 September 2026. The process includes essays, references, and often an interview, so give yourself real runway to make the case for why you specifically should be in that room in Beijing.
