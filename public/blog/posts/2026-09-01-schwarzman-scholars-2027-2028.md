Schwarzman Scholars places roughly 150 young leaders a year at Tsinghua University for a one-year master's in Global Affairs, taught in English alongside students from China, the US, and beyond. The programme covers tuition, room and board, a laptop, course materials, travel, and a $4,000 personal stipend, and it deliberately recruits outside the usual Ivy League pipeline, which is why African applicants with strong leadership records stand a real shot.

Founded by US private equity investor Stephen Schwarzman, the scholarship is run out of Tsinghua with an international board and is explicitly framed as training for people who will shape US-China and global relations over the next decades. For a Nigerian applicant, it is a rare, fully funded credential that opens doors in policy, finance, and technology circles most scholarships never touch, and unlike Rhodes or Chevening, it does not require a prior UK or US application to be in motion.
