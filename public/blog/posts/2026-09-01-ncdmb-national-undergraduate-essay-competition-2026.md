Now in its tenth year, the Nigerian Content Development and Monitoring Board's essay competition asks undergraduates to write on 'Promoting Manufacturing for Sustainable Local Content Development,' a topic tied directly to NCDMB's mandate of keeping oil and gas value where Nigerians can capture it. Winners split ₦2.2 million in cash, the top ten national finalists each get a laptop, and the winning essay gets published in the Board's Local Content Digest.

It costs nothing to enter beyond the essay itself, and it rewards clear thinking over connections or capital, which makes it one of the more accessible national competitions on this list. First and second year students who can write persuasively about industrial policy have a real shot at a prize most of their peers will never hear about in time to enter.
