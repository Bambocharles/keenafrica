The All On 2026 Off-Grid Energy Challenge is Nigeria's highest-value startup competition in the clean energy space, offering selected winners between **$200,000 and $1,000,000 in blended financing** (non-dilutive grants plus commercial instruments). Since 2018, All On has committed $3.6 million to 36 Nigerian companies, with those investments providing electricity access to over 150,000 Nigerians.

The 2026 edition prioritizes **Productive Use of Energy (PUE)** solutions — technologies that transform electricity access into economic output through agriculture processing, cold storage, healthcare delivery, and enterprise. It also targets mini-grids, solar home systems, energy efficiency innovations, and cold chain solutions. Winners receive technical assistance and investment-readiness coaching from the All On Hub team, on top of the capital.

To qualify, your company must be majority Nigerian-owned, legally registered and operational in Nigeria, and demonstrate a sustainable business model with a clear roadmap to serve underserved communities within 12–18 months. **Applications close 31 July 2026** — this deadline is critically imminent. If you are building in energy, this is the most relevant competition in Nigeria right now.
