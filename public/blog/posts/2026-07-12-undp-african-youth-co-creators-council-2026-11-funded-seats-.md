The UNDP African Youth Co-Creators Council is one of the most unusual opportunities in this edition: rather than attending a conference as a participant, you join as an advisor. UNDP is selecting just 11 young leaders from across Africa and the African diaspora to directly advise the United Nations Development Programme on its programming, policy, and initiatives across the continent. This is a governance and influence role, not a passive learning experience, you will have a genuine seat at a table that shapes development priorities for 1.4 billion Africans.

Participation is funded, covering the costs associated with Council engagement including relevant travel to meetings and convenings. For young Nigerians working in development, policy, fintech, health, education, or social innovation, a UNDP Council role is a career-defining credential and a real pipeline into international institutions, multilateral careers, and policy influence. Council members gain direct exposure to UNDP's senior leadership, global network, and strategic decision-making processes.

Applications close 31 July 2026. With only 11 seats available globally, competition is intense, but Nigeria has consistently produced candidates who reach the final stages of UNDP and UN selection processes. Focus your application on concrete impact: specific communities you have served, measurable outcomes you have produced, and a compelling articulation of what perspective you bring that others cannot.
