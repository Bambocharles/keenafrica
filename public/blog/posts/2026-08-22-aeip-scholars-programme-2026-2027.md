The Africa-Europe Innovation Platform selects five African and five European scholars each cycle, pairs them into cross-continental teams, and funds them to produce a joint research output addressing a shared development challenge. It is a rare structure: rather than competing for a single prize, you are matched with a European counterpart and resourced to build something together.

The application starts with an Expression of Interest built around a short abstract of your proposed research direction. Selection favours scholars whose work has a clear path to real-world application, not just academic novelty.

Winning pairs are sponsored to present at the AEIP Stakeholder Meeting in Europe in June 2027, and the top three pairs are invited to the Africa-Europe Innovation Dialogues 2027, a platform that puts early-career researchers in front of policymakers and funders who rarely take unsolicited meetings.
