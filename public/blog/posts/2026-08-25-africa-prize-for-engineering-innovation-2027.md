Run by the UK's Royal Academy of Engineering, the Africa Prize is the continent's largest award dedicated purely to engineering innovation, not just an idea, but hardware, products, and systems that solve real local problems. Total prize money across the cohort is £85,000: £50,000 to the overall winner, £10,000 each to three finalists, and £5,000 for a One-to-Watch award.

The real value is the year of commercialisation support that comes before the prize money: mentoring, investment readiness training, and a network of engineers who've taken hardware ideas to market across the continent. Applications run in two stages, so getting the first-stage submission in early gives you room to strengthen it if you're shortlisted.
