Organized by the National Association of Nigerian Students, NANSInnovate 2026 is built specifically for students who've already started something, not idea-stage pitches. You submit a business proposal (max 10 pages) showing what you've built and how funding would scale it, and the strongest entrants compete in a national pitch event for direct funding.

There's no entry fee at any stage, which sets it apart from many student competitions that charge for registration or mentorship add-ons. If you're a student already running a business on the side, this is a direct, low-friction shot at capital without giving up equity.
