AWS re/Start, delivered in Nigeria through Lonadek Global Services and the Ishk Tolaram Foundation, is a tuition-free, in-person program in Jibowu, Lagos that takes people with zero cloud background to job-ready in twelve weeks. It covers the AWS Certified Cloud Practitioner exam at no cost, alongside professional skills like resume building and interviewing that most technical bootcamps skip.

Cohorts run on a rolling basis, so there is no single hard deadline, but the tradeoff is a genuine full-time commitment: eight hours a day, Monday to Friday, plus a laptop and stable internet. Lonadek also connects graduates directly with employers afterward, which is the part free certificates alone rarely deliver.
