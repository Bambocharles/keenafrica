All On, a Shell-backed off-grid energy impact investor, has launched the 2026 Off-Grid Energy Challenge, targeting Nigerian entrepreneurs building commercially viable solutions in renewable energy, productive use technologies (PUE), energy efficiency, cold chain systems, mini-grids, and solar home systems. Since 2018, the programme has committed $3.6 million to 36 Nigerian-owned companies, delivering clean energy access to over 150,000 Nigerians. The 2026 edition dramatically raises the stakes.

Successful applicants receive between **$200,000 and $1,000,000 in blended funding**, structured as a combination of non-dilutive grants and commercial instruments. Beyond capital, winners receive technical assistance and investment-readiness support from the All On Hub team. Priority this year goes to productive use of energy (PUE) projects, solutions that turn electricity access into economic opportunity through agriculture processing, cold storage, and healthcare.

To qualify, your company must be majority Nigerian-owned and managed, legally registered, operating in Nigeria, and able to demonstrate innovation, a sustainable business model, and a clear plan to serve underserved communities within a 12–18 month investment period. Applications opened 2 July and **close 31 July 2026**, time is critically short. Apply immediately.
