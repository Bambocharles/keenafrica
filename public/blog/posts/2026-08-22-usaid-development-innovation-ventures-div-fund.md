DIV is one of the largest open-application funding mechanisms available to African innovators, structured in three stages so the amount of funding grows as your evidence base grows. Stage 1 offers $25,000 to $200,000 to test a new idea, Stage 2 offers $200,000 to $1.5 million to rigorously evaluate what is working, and Stage 3 offers up to $5 million to scale something that has already proven itself.

Applications are accepted on a rolling basis with no submission deadline, which means there is no reason to wait for a cohort to open. The application asks for a clear articulation of the problem, why your approach is more cost-effective than existing alternatives, and what evidence would prove it works.

Because DIV funds individuals and for-profits as well as nonprofits, it is open to Nigerian entrepreneurs building for-profit ventures with a genuine development angle, not just NGOs. The rolling structure rewards founders who apply as soon as they have something worth testing rather than waiting for a deadline that does not exist.
