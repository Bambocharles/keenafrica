YALCON brings together young delegates from across Africa for a day of talks, pitching, and networking around this year's theme, 'From Idea to Global Impact.' It's a smaller, community-built event rather than a corporate summit, which means more direct access to organizers, speakers, and fellow delegates than you'd get at a large trade conference.

Selected applicants get invited based on the leadership potential and social impact shown in their registration, and delegates who stand out are offered mentorship running from October through December after the conference. Registration is free, so the only cost is showing up in Enugu on 12 September ready to pitch.
