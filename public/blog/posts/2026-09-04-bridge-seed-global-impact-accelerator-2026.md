Bridge Seed Global runs a three-month accelerator for young African entrepreneurs, offering $5,000 in non-dilutive funding, meaning founders keep full ownership of their company, alongside structured coaching in business modelling, financial planning, and investment readiness. The program also connects founders directly to mentors and investor networks beyond the cohort itself.

Applications are reviewed on a rolling basis, so there is no fixed closing date, but earlier submissions get first access to interview slots and cohort spots. For a young Nigerian building in agriculture, health, education, or climate, this is a rare grant that does not ask for equity or a track record you don't yet have, just a real problem and a credible plan to solve it.
