Google, in partnership with Pluralsight and Andela, runs the Africa Developer Scholarship to give aspiring developers free, structured access to industry-recognised technical training. Participants pick one of three tracks, Associate Android Developer, Google Cloud Engineering, or Mobile Web Specialist, and get hands-on labs, projects, and skill assessments through Pluralsight Skills at no cost.

The programme has run for multiple cohorts and has sponsored tens of thousands of African developers to date. Top performers in the first phase advance to full nanodegree scholarships worth over $2,000, which is a real credential upgrade for anyone building a case for a first developer job or freelance client work.

There's no application fee, and the deadline for this round is 31 August 2026. Because it's genuinely free and doesn't require an existing portfolio, this is one of the most accessible entry points into cloud or mobile development available to Nigerian youth right now.
