Knight-Hennessy Scholars funds up to three years of graduate study at Stanford across any discipline, medicine, law, engineering, business, education, and more, alongside a leadership development program that puts scholars from every continent in the same cohort. It is one of the largest fully endowed graduate scholarships in the world, and it does not require you to already have a Stanford admission before you apply; the two applications run concurrently.

For a Nigerian applicant with strong academic results and a track record of initiative, whether that is a startup, research, or community leadership, this is a route into one of the most resourced universities on earth without the tuition bill. The application closes 6 October 2026 at 1pm Pacific Time, and you must also submit your Stanford graduate program application by 1 December 2026 to remain eligible.
