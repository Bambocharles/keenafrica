AWS re/Start is Amazon Web Services' global workforce development programme, delivered in Nigeria through local partners including Lonadek Global Services and EdoJobs, with support from the Ishk Tolaram Foundation. Over 12 weeks of full-time, in-person training, participants learn cloud fundamentals through scenario-based learning and hands-on labs, alongside professional skills like resume building, interviewing, and workplace communication.

What makes re/Start distinctive is that it deliberately doesn't require a technology background or degree, it's built for people who are motivated but haven't had access to formal tech training. Graduates prepare for the AWS Certified Cloud Practitioner exam with free access to practice and full exams, giving them both a credential and job-readiness support.

Apply through the official AWS re/Start application page, which routes you to the current learner application for your country. Cohort intake dates vary and open periodically through the Nigerian partner organisations, so apply now to be considered for the next available cohort.
