The AU-EU Youth Action Lab is a joint African Union and European Union initiative that puts serious money, 30,000 to 40,000 euros in its Innovation Grant track, behind youth-led organizations rather than individuals with an idea, which means it rewards teams that have already built something real. A separate Cooperation Grant of 50,000 euros funds year-long partnerships between an African and a European organization, useful if your Nigerian outfit already has a European counterpart worth formalizing.

The bar is organizational: you need a registered entity governed by young people, not just a WhatsApp group with ambition. Applicants can only submit to one grant track at a time, so decide early which category fits before the 23 September deadline.
