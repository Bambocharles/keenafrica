The GREAT Scholarships are a joint initiative between the UK Government and the British Council, offering £10,000 toward tuition fees for a one-year postgraduate programme at universities across the United Kingdom. For 2026–27, Nigerian students can apply to scholarships at top institutions including Brunel University, Cranfield University, Imperial College London, University College London (UCL), University of Warwick, University of Bath, University of Southampton, and others.

Each participating university manages its own selection process, with courses available across engineering, business, law, public health, sustainability, and technology. The scholarship is highly targeted, you apply directly to the institution of your choice after securing an offer of admission. This is not a single centralised application, which means your chances are multiplied if you apply to multiple institutions simultaneously.

As a GREAT Scholar, you also become part of a cohort of UK-based Nigerian scholars with networking events and ambassador opportunities. Check the British Council Nigeria GREAT Scholarships page for the full list of institutions and their individual deadlines, several are still actively accepting applications as of July 2026. This is real money that cuts the cost of a UK postgraduate degree significantly, from a government-backed programme with decades of credibility.
