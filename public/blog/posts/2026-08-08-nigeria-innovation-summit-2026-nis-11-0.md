Now in its 11th edition, the Nigeria Innovation Summit convenes founders, policymakers, investors, and academics at the Shell Hall, Muson Centre in Lagos for a single, intense day under the theme 'Innovating in the Age of AI.' Expect keynote talks, NiX Talks showcasing emerging technologies, startup pitches, workshops, and fireside chats with people actually building and funding Nigeria's tech ecosystem.

What sets NIS apart from bigger, pricier tech conferences is that it has stayed genuinely free and open, which matters if you're a student or early-stage founder who wants access without a corporate budget behind you. It's also a compact, single-day event, so the networking is dense rather than diluted across a week.

Registration is free at innovationsummit.ng and the summit holds on 6 October 2026. If you're building anything AI-adjacent, this is one of the highest-density rooms in West Africa to be in that day.
