NiYA Startup Pitch 2.0 is the second round of the Nigerian Youth Academy's flagship funding challenge, backed by the Federal Ministry of Youth Development and launched from the Presidential Villa. It is selecting 100 startups to receive a share of ₦100 million in grant funding, alongside technical training and enterprise support designed to get very early founders investment ready rather than just cash rich.

Applications are reviewed on a rolling basis, so unlike most government-linked grants there is no single cliff-edge deadline, but early, complete applications get first look. For a founder building outside Lagos or Abuja without an existing investor network, this is one of the few non-dilutive routes to capital that does not require years of revenue history first.
