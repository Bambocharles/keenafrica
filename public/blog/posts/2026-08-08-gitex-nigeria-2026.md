GITEX Nigeria brings the globally recognised GITEX technology exhibition to Abuja and Lagos, organised by KAOUN International in partnership with Nigeria's Federal Ministry of Communications, Innovation and Digital Economy and NITDA. The Abuja leg opens with a Government Leadership and AI Summit, while the Lagos leg hosts the Tech Expo, Future Economy Conference, and a dedicated Startup Festival where founders pitch to investors and connect with global tech leaders.

For a young Nigerian builder, this is a rare chance to be in the room with ministers, regulators, and international investors without paying a cent for access. Registration is completely free for a limited period, covering both venues, all conference sessions, exhibition floor access, and networking events.

Run from 31 August to 3 September 2026, GITEX Nigeria is worth attending even if you're not exhibiting: the hallway conversations and investor meetups are often where real deals start. Register early since free passes are capacity-limited.
