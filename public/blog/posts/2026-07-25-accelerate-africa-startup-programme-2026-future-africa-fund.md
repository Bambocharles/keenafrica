The Accelerate Africa Startup Programme 2026 is built for bold African founders tackling the continent's most pressing challenges through technology. There is no application fee and no upfront equity required to apply. The programme provides hands-on expert guidance, investor access, and structured founder support — and selected startups gain direct access to the Future Africa fund, with potential investment ranging from USD $250,000 to $500,000 in follow-on funding.

Accelerate Africa targets founders at the earliest stages who have a clear thesis, a product in development or early traction, and the ambition to build globally competitive businesses rooted in Africa. The programme is sector-agnostic but tends to gravitate toward startups with technology at the core — whether fintech, healthtech, edtech, agritech, or infrastructure. If you are pre-revenue or early-revenue and need the structure, mentorship, and investor exposure to take your next leap, this programme is worth a serious application.
