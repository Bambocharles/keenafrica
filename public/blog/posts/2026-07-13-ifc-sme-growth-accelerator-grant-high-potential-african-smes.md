The International Finance Corporation (IFC), the private-sector arm of the World Bank Group, runs the SME Growth Accelerator to address Africa's estimated $331 billion SME funding gap. This is a non-dilutive grant, meaning your business retains full ownership, specifically targeting companies that have already demonstrated traction but need catalytic capital to expand operations, increase production capacity, strengthen distribution, or improve business infrastructure.

The application process expects applicants to present a clear market opportunity, a sustainable business model, and measurable growth projections. Strong financial modelling and a practical use-of-funds strategy significantly improve approval chances. The program is open across the African continent, making it accessible to Nigerian SMEs across sectors including agriculture, manufacturing, services, and technology.

With a deadline of **15 November 2026**, you have time to build your application carefully. The IFC's backing adds credibility: selected businesses often gain additional visibility within the World Bank Group's wider ecosystem of investors and development finance partners. This is a serious grant for businesses that are past the idea stage and ready to grow.
