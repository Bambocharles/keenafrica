The DAAD In-Country/In-Region (ICIR) Programme is a unique fully funded scholarship that enables Nigerian and Sub-Saharan African graduates to pursue Master's degrees at excellent universities within Africa — including FUNAAB in Abeokuta, Nigeria — rather than requiring travel to Germany. The programme specifically targets individuals who plan careers in research and teaching at higher education institutions in Sub-Saharan Africa, making it ideal for aspiring academic leaders.

Funding covers the full regular duration of the Master's programme (generally up to two years), tuition fees, stipend, and support costs. Female applicants and candidates from less-privileged regions are especially encouraged to apply. The 2026/2027 intake has an application deadline of **29 April 2026** — check the DAAD scholarship database now to confirm whether this specific call remains open or if new 2027 calls have been published, as additional openings are announced in autumn 2026.

Applications are submitted directly through each host institution following the specific procedures listed in the call for applications on the DAAD portal.
