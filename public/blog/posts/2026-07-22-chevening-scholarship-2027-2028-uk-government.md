Funded by the UK's Foreign, Commonwealth and Development Office (FCDO), the Chevening Scholarship brings outstanding emerging leaders to the UK for a one-year taught Master's degree at any eligible UK university of their choice. The package covers full tuition fees regardless of cost, a monthly living allowance, economy return airfare, an arrival allowance, a departure allowance, and visa fees, a total value typically exceeding £30,000–£50,000 depending on course and institution.

The 2027/2028 application window opens in **August 2026** and closes on **6 October 2026**. Nigerian applicants must apply to three different UK universities and secure at least one unconditional offer by a later deadline in the cycle. Nigeria is consistently one of the largest recipients of Chevening awards globally, and Nigerian doctors, lawyers, engineers, and entrepreneurs have used the scholarship as a springboard for continental impact.

The application is rigorous: four 500-word essays covering leadership, networking, studying in the UK, and career plan; three university choices; and two references. The Chevening portal is your only application route. Start drafting essays and approaching referees now, the window is weeks away.
