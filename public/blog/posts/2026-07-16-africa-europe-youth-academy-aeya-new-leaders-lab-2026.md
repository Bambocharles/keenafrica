The Africa-Europe Youth Academy's New Leaders Lab is a flagship EU-Africa leadership and innovation programme, fully funded for selected participants. The programme brings together ambitious young leaders from 19 African countries, Nigeria is among the eligible nations, for an intensive training experience centred on entrepreneurship, policy, and Africa-Europe collaboration. Participants engage in structured leadership modules, mentorship sessions with senior practitioners, and cross-cultural dialogue with their European peers.

Beyond the immediate training, the New Leaders Lab is a gateway into a broader network of African and European young leaders who are actively shaping the discourse on trade, innovation, climate, and governance between the two continents. The programme is backed by EU/Africa institutional support, lending it credibility and post-programme follow-through.

The application deadline is July 19, 2026, just days away. To apply, visit Opportunities For Africans (opportunitiesforafricans.com) and search for the AEYA New Leaders Lab 2026. The application assesses your background, vision, and alignment with the programme's focus areas. If you are a Nigerian leader, founder, or changemaker between 18 and 30, this is one of the most direct pathways to structured Europe-Africa leadership networks currently open.
