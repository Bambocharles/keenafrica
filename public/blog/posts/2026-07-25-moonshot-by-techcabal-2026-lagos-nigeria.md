Moonshot by TechCabal is now in its fourth edition and has established itself as the most important technology conference on the African continent. The 2025 edition drew 6,000 attendees from 39 countries across 6 continents. The 2026 edition takes place October 28–29 at the National Theatre, Lagos — right in Nigeria's backyard — under the theme 'Courage & Conviction: Building for a New World.' The conference will run nine content tracks: Emerging Tech, Future of Commerce, Big Tech & Enterprise, Government & Policy, FUEL: The Investors Conference, Creative Economy, Clean Tech & Climate, Startup Festival, and Entering Tech.

For Nigerian founders and builders, Moonshot is a rare opportunity to be in the same room as investors who have collectively deployed over $5 billion, operators from OpenAI Africa and Flutterwave, and Nigeria's SEC leadership — all in Lagos. The conference's TC Battlefield is a live startup pitch competition that has given multiple African startups international visibility and real funding conversations. The 'Entering Tech' track is specifically designed for young professionals still finding their footing in the ecosystem.
