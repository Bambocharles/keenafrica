Draper Richards Kaplan Foundation is a venture philanthropy firm that funds early-stage social entrepreneurs the way a venture capital firm funds startups: with real capital, hands-on support, and no restrictions on how the money is spent. Selected organisations receive up to $300,000 over three years, alongside a board-level relationship with DRK partners who actively help with strategy, hiring, and follow-on fundraising.

DRK funds ventures across education, health, environment, and economic development, and it explicitly includes Africa among its regions of focus. This is not a small grant for a pilot project, it's designed for founders who already have a working model and are ready to scale it into something structurally bigger.

Applications are accepted year-round with no fixed deadline, submitted through a short online form with your executive summary, pitch deck, or business plan. Because there's no application fee and no deadline pressure, this is worth applying to the moment your venture has real traction to show, rather than waiting for a call for applications.
