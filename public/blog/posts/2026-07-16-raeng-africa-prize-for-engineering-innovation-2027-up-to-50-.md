The Royal Academy of Engineering Africa Prize for Engineering Innovation is the continent's most prestigious and highest-value engineering competition, awarding a total prize pool of £85,000. The winner receives £50,000 outright, three runners-up each receive £10,000, and an additional £5,000 'One-to-Watch' award is chosen by the live audience at the final. But the money is only part of the value — the real prize is the eight-month intensive programme of business training, expert mentoring (including financial management, IP protection, market analysis, and sector-specific engineering mentoring), and access to the Royal Academy's global network of engineers and investors.

The prize covers innovations across any engineering discipline broadly defined: agritech, biotechnology, ICT, civil engineering, energy, medical engineering, and more. Your innovation must be based in sub-Saharan Africa, be at an early stage of commercialisation, and have a credible business plan. Hardware entries need a working prototype; software/app entries need an MVP with evidence of traction. The two-stage application process begins now, with Stage 1 submissions due by September 8, 2026 at 4:00 PM UTC+1.

Nigeria has a strong track record at the Africa Prize — the competitive innovation ecosystem in Lagos and beyond has produced strong previous applicants. If you are building an engineering-based solution to a local challenge with real market potential, this is the programme to enter. The 12-year legacy of the prize, with 165+ supported businesses from 22 countries, makes this one of the most credible and impactful innovation competitions on the continent.
