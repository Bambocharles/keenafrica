The UNDP African Youth Co-Creators Council is not just a conference; it is a seat at the table. The United Nations Development Programme is selecting 11 young leaders from across Africa and the African diaspora to formally advise on UNDP's programmes and policies across the continent. This is a structured leadership engagement, not a passive attendance opportunity. Selected members contribute directly to shaping how one of the world's largest development organisations responds to Africa's most critical challenges.

Participation costs are fully covered, and council members gain direct engagement with senior UNDP officials, global policymakers, and fellow African changemakers. The visibility and network value of being a UNDP-selected Co-Creator is significant, both for individual credibility and for organisations seeking to influence development outcomes at scale.

Applications are open now and close July 31, 2026. The selection process is competitive and values demonstrated impact, clarity of vision, and the ability to represent diverse African perspectives.
