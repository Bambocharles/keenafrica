The Rhodes Scholarship for West Africa is a fully funded postgraduate award covering Oxford course fees, a living stipend of £20,400 per year, the Oxford application fee, UK visa and Immigration Health Surcharge costs, two economy-class flights, and a settling-in allowance. Three scholarships are available for the entire West Africa constituency, which includes Nigeria, Ghana, Senegal, Liberia, Sierra Leone, Niger, Togo, and others, making every application a genuine competition for one of the world's most recognised academic honours.

The West Africa constituency was formally established by the Rhodes Trust in 2017, building on a legacy of national scholarships that previously included standalone awards for Nigeria and Ghana. Interviews for the 2027 cycle will be held in Abuja, Accra, or Lagos in November 2026, with travel and accommodation within West Africa provided for finalists. The application itself requires a personal statement (up to 1,000 words), an academic transcript and statement of proposed study, a full CV, and three confidential references, all due by 23:59 GMT on 27 August 2026.

This is the scholarship you apply for when you want to change not just your career but the trajectory of your country. Selectors look for intellectual ability, leadership character, commitment to service, and physical vigour, not a perfect GPA in isolation. Past Nigerian Rhodes Scholars have gone on to roles in government, law, medicine, policy, and global institutions. If you have a first-class or strong 2:1 honours degree, a record of leadership outside the classroom, and a clear vision for your Oxford studies, start your application now.
