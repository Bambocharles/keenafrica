Commonwealth Shared Scholarships are funded by the UK Foreign, Commonwealth & Development Office (FCDO) and delivered by the Association of Commonwealth Universities (ACU). The programme targets candidates from eligible lower-income Commonwealth countries — Nigeria included — with an explicit focus on development impact. The scholarship covers full tuition, airfare, living allowance, and thesis grant for one-year taught Master's programmes at UK universities.

Eligible disciplines are tightly linked to development themes: science and technology for development, renewable energy, public health, agriculture and food security, educational development, and economics. This is intentional: the Commonwealth Shared Scholarship is designed to fund the next generation of development practitioners, not generalist graduates. If your field aligns with Nigeria's structural challenges, this is one of the strongest scholarships you can pursue.

Applications are submitted through the CSC portal and require a strong personal statement, two academic references, and proof that you meet the financial means criteria. The 2026/27 deadline for Nigerian applicants typically falls around October–November, mirroring the Chevening cycle.
