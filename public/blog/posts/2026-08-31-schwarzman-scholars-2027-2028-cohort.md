Schwarzman Scholars is one of the most competitive fully funded master's programs in the world, built to put rising leaders from across the globe in direct contact with China's role in shaping the next century. Scholars live and study together at Tsinghua University for a year, take classes in leadership, economics, and international affairs, and graduate into a lifelong network of Schwarzman alumni now working across government, finance, and technology.

The program covers everything: tuition, room and board, a laptop, travel to and from Beijing, and an in-country study trip. Late applications are never accepted, so if you have an undergraduate degree and want a credential that opens doors in both East and West, this is worth clearing your calendar for before the 9 September deadline.
