Microsoft Virtual Training Days (MVTDs) are free, instructor-led online training events that Microsoft runs continuously throughout the year, covering Azure, AI, Security, Data, and Microsoft 365 technologies. After attending a qualifying full session, typically split across two half-days, Microsoft emails you a 50% discount voucher for the corresponding Fundamentals certification exam (AZ-900 Azure Fundamentals, AI-900 Azure AI Fundamentals, SC-900 Security Fundamentals, DP-900 Data Fundamentals, and others). The training is 100% free, requiring only a free Microsoft account to register.

For Nigerian professionals, the AZ-900 (Azure Fundamentals) is the recommended starting credential, it is the most recognised entry-level cloud certification globally, valued by employers in Nigeria, the UK, the US, Canada, and beyond. The AI-900 (Azure AI Fundamentals) is increasingly in demand as AI adoption accelerates across industries. Both can be studied using the free learning paths on Microsoft Learn (learn.microsoft.com), mapped directly to exam objectives. You will typically receive your 50% discount voucher within 5–10 business days of the event's conclusion, and you have 90 days to schedule and sit the exam.

New events are added to the Microsoft Events portal every month, visit events.microsoft.com and filter by 'Microsoft Virtual Training Days' to see upcoming dates and register. Popular sessions fill quickly, so register early. This is a legitimate, repeatable pathway: complete the training, get the voucher, pass the exam, and move to the next certification. No cost to attend, no application process, just show up, learn, and earn.
