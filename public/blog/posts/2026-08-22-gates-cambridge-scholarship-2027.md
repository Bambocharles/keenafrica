Gates Cambridge is one of the most competitive postgraduate scholarships in the world, and it is explicitly open to Nigerian applicants alongside a short list of other African countries. It funds any subject at any level, from a one-year Master's to a PhD, at one of the world's oldest and best-resourced universities.

There is no separate scholarship application: you apply for your Cambridge course through the standard university portal and tick the box indicating you want to be considered for Gates funding within that same process. The University's application portal for 2027 entry reopens in September 2026, with course deadlines falling between 8 December 2026 and 6 January 2027 depending on subject.

The scholarship covers the full cost of studying at Cambridge: tuition, a maintenance allowance, and one return flight per year. Given Cambridge's academic reputation, this is one of the highest-leverage scholarships a Nigerian student can put on a CV, and the lead time between now and the January deadline is enough to build a genuinely strong application rather than rush one.
