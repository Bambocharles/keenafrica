Launched at Google I/O on May 19, 2026, the Build with Gemini XPRIZE is a 90-day global hackathon backed by Google and organised by XPRIZE, the world's leading operator of large-scale incentive competitions. The total prize pool is $2 million, distributed across 25 winners, including a $1.75 million grand prize pool for the top 5 finalists, 15 runner-up prizes of $50,000 each, and five category prizes. This is not a prototype competition: submissions are judged on real users, real revenue, and AI running in production.

The five competition categories are Education & Human Potential, Entrepreneurship & Job Creation, Small Business Services, Money & Financial Access, and Professional Services. Participants build using Google's full AI stack, Gemini, Google AI Studio, Antigravity, Cloud Run, Stitch, and Flow, all of which are provided. For Nigerian builders who have been experimenting with AI tools, this is the competition that pays you to ship a real product, not just demo a prototype. Submissions are due August 17, 2026; live finals are September 25 in Los Angeles.

There is no purchase or payment necessary to enter or win. Register on the official Devpost platform at xprize.devpost.com, review the full rules at geminixprize.com/rules, and start building. The Nigerian tech community has the talent to win in categories like Financial Access and Entrepreneurship, but only if you submit before the deadline.
