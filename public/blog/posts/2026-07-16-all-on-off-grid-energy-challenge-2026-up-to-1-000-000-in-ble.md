The 2026 All On Off-Grid Energy Challenge is a landmark funding opportunity for Nigerian clean energy entrepreneurs, offering successful applicants between $200,000 and $1,000,000 in blended funding, combining non-dilutive grants with commercial financing instruments. All On, an independent impact investor seeded by Shell, has committed $3.6 million to 36 Nigerian-owned companies since 2018 through previous editions, helping bring clean energy to over 150,000 Nigerians.

This year's edition specifically prioritises solutions in productive use of energy (PUE), including agricultural processing, cold storage, healthcare services, solar home systems, and mini-grids, as well as broader renewable energy and off-grid applications. The award is not just capital: successful applicants also receive technical assistance and investment-readiness support from the All On Hub team, giving early-stage founders genuine scale-up infrastructure.

To be eligible, your company must be majority Nigerian-owned and managed, legally registered and operating in Nigeria, and must demonstrate a credible plan to serve unserved communities within a 12–18 month investment period. Applications opened on July 2 and close on July 31, 2026, this window is tight. Submit early to avoid technical issues, and ensure your business plan, innovation narrative, and financial projections are watertight before you apply.
