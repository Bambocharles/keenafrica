Data Science Nigeria, one of Nigeria's most impactful tech capacity-building organisations, has partnered with Microsoft to run the **Microsoft Elevate AI Developers Training 2026**, a fully funded programme that prepares Nigerian learners for globally recognised Microsoft certification exams at zero cost to participants. The programme covers artificial intelligence, cloud computing (Azure), data analytics, cybersecurity, and business intelligence, all areas where Microsoft certifications carry significant market value for Nigerian tech professionals seeking remote work, international employment, or startup credibility.

Participants get access to industry-standard training modules on Microsoft Learn, structured learning paths, and preparation resources for Microsoft certification exams that are recognised by organisations worldwide. For developers and students who want Azure AI Fundamentals (AI-900), Azure Data Fundamentals (DP-900), or security-related certifications, this program provides the training infrastructure and, for qualifying participants, exam voucher support.

Applications are reviewed on a rolling basis, check the Data Science Nigeria portal for the current active cohort and enrol immediately. Data Science Nigeria has a proven track record of running large-scale, high-quality tech training programmes across Nigeria, including in partnership with Google, IBM, and now Microsoft.
