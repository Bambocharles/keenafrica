ALX Africa is one of the continent's most impactful tech training ecosystems, offering programmes in Software Engineering, Data Science, AI Career Essentials, Cloud Computing, Virtual Assistance, and more. Through its Mastercard Foundation partnership, eligible African learners can access the full ALX programme portfolio for as little as $5 per month, a fraction of what equivalent training costs globally. For learners who qualify for fully sponsored places, programme costs are waived entirely (a one-time administration fee for confirmed enrolment may apply). ALX operates in-person hubs in Lagos, Accra, Nairobi, Cairo, Casablanca, Kigali, and Johannesburg, alongside fully remote tracks.

The 12-month Software Engineering Programme is ALX's flagship offering, an intensive bootcamp that covers C programming, Python, JavaScript, full-stack development, DevOps, and a chosen specialisation (Front-End, Back-End, Blockchain, AR/VR, or DevOps). Graduates receive an ALX Software Engineering certificate, a GitHub portfolio, and access to The ROOM, ALX's talent placement network used by Microsoft, Andela, Flutterwave, MTN, and African startups. The AI Career Essentials programme is a shorter, non-technical pathway for professionals in any field who want practical AI fluency, covering prompt engineering, AI ethics, and AI tools for workplace productivity.

Applications to all ALX programmes are open year-round on a rolling basis, with cohort starts typically in January, April/May, August, and November. The next major cohort intake is expected in August 2026, apply early for faster review. For Nigerians in Akure without access to Lagos-based hubs, the fully remote tracks make ALX equally accessible. Visit the ALX programmes page, use the programme finder tool to identify the right track for your goals, and submit your application, the process takes about 90 minutes including an English proficiency and logical-reasoning assessment.
