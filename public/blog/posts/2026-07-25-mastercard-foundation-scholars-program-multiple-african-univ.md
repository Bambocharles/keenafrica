The Mastercard Foundation Scholars Program is one of the largest scholarship initiatives on the African continent, partnering with over 30 universities across Africa, Canada, and the US — including the University of Lagos, African Leadership University, University of Ghana, McGill, and UC Berkeley. The programme covers full tuition, living expenses, books, travel, and in many cases career development stipends. It was explicitly built to serve the most talented young Africans who cannot access higher education due to financial constraints.

What sets Mastercard Foundation apart from other scholarships is its wraparound support model. Beyond funding, scholars receive mentorship, leadership training, career placement support, and access to a continent-wide alumni network. The programme's alumni are now working across government, tech, social enterprise, and finance in more than 50 countries. For young Nigerians who are academically strong but cannot self-finance a postgraduate degree, this is among the most generous scholarships available.

Deadlines are staggered across partner institutions — some windows open in August 2026, others in September and October. You must apply directly through your chosen partner university's scholarships portal, not through the Mastercard Foundation website centrally. Visit the Scholars Program page, identify partner institutions aligned with your field, and apply to the ones whose windows are open now.
