Microsoft Virtual Training Days (MVTDs) are free, live, instructor-led online events run by Microsoft throughout the year. After attending a qualifying session, typically split across two half-days, you receive a 50% discount voucher for the relevant Microsoft certification exam, delivered to your email within 5–10 days of the event. The voucher is applied directly to your Microsoft Learn account and redeemed through Pearson VUE when you schedule your exam. Vouchers are valid for 90 days from issuance, giving you a clear study-and-sit window.

The most in-demand certifications covered by Virtual Training Days in 2026 include **AZ-900 (Azure Fundamentals)**, **AI-900 (Azure AI Fundamentals)**, **SC-900 (Security, Compliance & Identity Fundamentals)**, **AB-900 (Microsoft 365 Fundamentals)**, and select associate-level exams including SC-200 and DP-300. These credentials are increasingly listed on job descriptions at Nigerian tech companies, banks, telecoms, and multinationals operating on the continent. For a Keen Africa learner, AZ-900 or AI-900 is the ideal starting point, both are achievable in 40–60 hours of self-study on Microsoft Learn, which is entirely free.

To access your 50% voucher: register for an upcoming event at the Microsoft Events portal linked below, filter by 'Virtual Training Days', choose a session relevant to your target exam, attend the live event fully (typically 100% attendance is required for one track), and wait for the voucher email. New events are added monthly and registration is first-come, first-served, popular sessions fill quickly. Once you have your voucher, complete your exam on Microsoft Learn's free learning path and book your Pearson VUE slot before the voucher expires.
