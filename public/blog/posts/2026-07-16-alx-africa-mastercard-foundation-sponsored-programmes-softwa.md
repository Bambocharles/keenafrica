ALX Africa, in partnership with the Mastercard Foundation, offers sponsored training places in some of the most in-demand tech programmes on the continent, including Software Engineering, AWS Cloud Computing, Data Analytics, and AI Career Essentials. Through this partnership, eligible learners can access world-class programme training with all costs fully waived, plus access to a peer learning community, global university partners, and in-person tech hubs in Lagos.

ALX is part of the African Leadership Group ecosystem, founded by Fred Swaniker, and has trained tens of thousands of young Africans across eight cities. The programmes are rigorous, Software Engineering, for example, requires 70–80 hours per week of commitment, but graduates walk away with globally competitive credentials and access to The ROOM, ALX's talent placement network connecting graduates directly with international employers.

Applications to all ALX programmes are open throughout the year on a rolling basis. To apply for a sponsored (fully funded) place, visit the ALX Nigeria page, register your interest, and motivate for a sponsored placement. You must meet country-of-origin and age requirements for sponsored access. This is one of the fastest legitimate pathways for a young Nigerian to build verifiable, employer-ready tech skills without needing to pay upfront.
