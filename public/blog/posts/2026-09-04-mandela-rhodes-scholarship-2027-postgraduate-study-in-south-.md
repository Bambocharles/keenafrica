The Mandela Rhodes Foundation funds postgraduate study, Honours or Master's, at any South African university, and pairs it with a leadership curriculum built around Nelson Mandela's legacy: reconciliation, education, and entrepreneurship. Scholars join a pan-African network that runs well beyond graduation, with the Foundation actively investing in scholars' ventures and projects after the scholarship year ends.

Applications for the 2027 intake are open now and close 14 April 2027, giving Nigerian applicants ample runway to prepare a strong application and secure a university offer. It suits graduates who want a rigorous postgraduate qualification without leaving the continent, plus a leadership credential that carries real weight across Africa's development and business sectors.
