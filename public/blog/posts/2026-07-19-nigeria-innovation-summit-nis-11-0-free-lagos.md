The Nigeria Innovation Summit (NIS) 11.0 is the 11th edition of West Africa's largest and most celebrated innovation event, scheduled for October 6, 2026, at the Shell Hall, Muson Centre, Lagos. Organised by InnovationHub Africa, the theme this year is 'Innovating in the Age of AI' — exploring how Nigeria and Africa can harness AI to drive economic growth and compete on the global stage. The programme features NiX Talks (Nigeria Innovation Experience Talks), startup pitches, workshops, fireside chats, showcases of emerging technologies, and the 2026 Nigeria Innovation Awards.

Attendance is completely free. Past editions have drawn over 15,000 delegates, 240 speakers and panelists, 100 tertiary institutions and research centres, 40 government ministries, and participants from 42 countries. By registering in advance, you secure your seat and gain full access to all Summit materials. The event is also live-streamed for those who cannot attend in person. Previous attendees have highlighted the Innovation Tour — selected delegates visit tech experience centres operated by partners like Microsoft — as one of the most impactful elements of the experience.

For any young Nigerian builder, NIS 11.0 is not optional — it is where the ecosystem gathers. The access to investors, high-net-worth individuals, potential co-founders, and industry leaders is unmatched in terms of cost-to-value. Come with business cards, a sharp one-minute pitch, and genuine curiosity. Register early on the official website to guarantee your place.
