The IFC SME Growth Accelerator is one of the largest and most credible non-dilutive grant programmes currently open to African businesses. Funded by the International Finance Corporation (a member of the World Bank Group), it supports high-potential small and medium-sized enterprises seeking to scale operations, enter new markets, and create quality employment. Grant funding ranges from USD 50,000 to USD 1.5 million, depending on the nature and scale of the proposed expansion. Eligible expenditures include working capital, pilot deployments, technical assistance, climate and ESG-related investments, equipment and technology procurement, and market research.

Beyond the cash grant, selected SMEs receive mentorship from experienced industry experts, access to IFC's global business and investment networks, and opportunities for follow-on financing — arguably as valuable as the grant itself for ambitious founders looking to go beyond early-stage capital. The geographic scope covers Africa, Asia, and Latin America, and Nigerian SMEs operating in sectors from fintech to agritech to clean energy are eligible.

The online application takes approximately 15 minutes to complete, and decisions are typically returned within 4–6 weeks of submission. The deadline is 15 November 2026, but applying early is strategically smart — earlier submissions allow more time for due diligence and reduce the risk of pipeline congestion. Your business must demonstrate strong growth potential, a scalable model, sound governance, and a clear plan for how the funding will generate measurable economic impact. If your SME has traction and is ready to scale, this is one of the most serious funding windows open right now.
