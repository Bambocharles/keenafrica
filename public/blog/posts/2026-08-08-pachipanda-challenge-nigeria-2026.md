Now in its third edition, the Nigeria PachiPanda Challenge is run by MTN Nigeria in partnership with the Worldwide Fund for Nature, the Nigeria Conservation Foundation, UNDP Nigeria, and Deloitte Nigeria. It's built for young people and youth-led SMEs tackling three focus areas: pollution and waste management, climate change and greenhouse gas reduction, and community-led conservation, with up to N10 million in prizes on offer.

What makes this challenge worth entering even at the idea stage is the boot camp: shortlisted teams go through an intensive training and mentorship period before the final pitch event, which means you leave with a sharper business regardless of whether you win. The final pitch event runs from 19 to 22 October 2026.

Applications opened 29 July 2026 and close 28 August 2026, submitted free of charge via the official MTN PachiPanda page. If you're building anything in clean energy, recycling, or conservation tech in Nigeria, this is a well-funded, well-mentored place to test it.
