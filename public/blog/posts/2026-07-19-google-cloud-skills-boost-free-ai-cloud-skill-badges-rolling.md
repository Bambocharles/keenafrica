Google Cloud Skills Boost (now rebranded as Google Skills) is the most technically rigorous free learning platform Google operates — and it is available to any Nigerian with internet access right now. When you start a lab on the platform, you receive a live Google Cloud environment with real credentials, real APIs, and real infrastructure. You are not clicking through a simulation. As of 2026, over 300 courses offer a free skill badge or completion badge, covering roles in AI/ML, DevOps, data engineering, security, and cloud architecture.

The GEAR programme offers 35 monthly free learning credits to all registered users — enough to access core learning paths at no cost. University students can request 200 Google Skills credits through the Student programme. Skill badges (the more valuable credential, requiring you to pass a timed challenge lab without step-by-step instructions) prove hands-on competence and are directly shareable to LinkedIn as verified credentials from Google. Recruiters filter for them. The free learning paths also prepare you for Google's paid Professional Cloud certifications ($200 each), which are among the most in-demand cloud credentials globally.

For Nigerian tech professionals, cloud certifications are not optional extras — they are the primary evidence of cloud capability in a market where employers in banking, fintech, and telecoms are actively migrating to cloud infrastructure. AWS, Azure, and GCP certifications are frequently listed as required qualifications in cloud and DevOps roles. Start with the Introduction to Generative AI path or the Google Cloud Fundamentals path, earn your first skill badge, put it on your LinkedIn profile, and build from there. It costs nothing and the opportunity cost of not starting is significant.
