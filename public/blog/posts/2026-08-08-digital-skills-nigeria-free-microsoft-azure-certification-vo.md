Digital Skills Nigeria is a skills empowerment platform run by the Federal Ministry of Communications, Innovation and Digital Economy in partnership with Microsoft and other institutions, built to give Nigerians access to cutting-edge digital training without the usual cost barrier. Courses span Azure fundamentals, Azure DevOps, data science with Azure Machine Learning, and AI skilling tracks.

The path to a free certification voucher is straightforward but requires follow-through: register for your course of choice, complete the learning material, and score above 60% on the mock exam by the 27th of the month. Qualifying candidates are compiled into a monthly list and notified by email, then given access to a proctor exam preparatory class before sitting the real certification exam for free.

Because the cycle runs monthly, there's no reason to wait for a special cohort announcement, you can start today and be in next month's voucher list. A Microsoft Azure certification is one of the more globally portable credentials a young Nigerian can add to a CV at zero cost.
