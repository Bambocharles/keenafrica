Schwarzman Scholars 2027–2028 (Tsinghua University, Beijing — Global/US Track)

Deadline 9 September 2026
Eligibility Open to all nationalities (except mainland China/HK/Macau/Taiwan applicants); must be 18–28 years old as of 1 August 2027 and hold an undergraduate degree by that date.
Apply now →

Schwarzman Scholars is a fully funded master’s programme at Tsinghua University in Beijing, one of China’s most prestigious universities. The scholarship covers tuition and fees, room and board on the Schwarzman College campus, round-trip travel to and from Beijing, study tours across China, books and supplies, a laptop, health insurance, and a personal stipend — all costs handled. Up to 200 scholars are selected annually from an intensely competitive global pool, and Nigeria has featured in past cohorts. No Chinese language proficiency is required; all instruction is in English.

The programme concentrates on three disciplines: Public Policy, Economics and Business, and International Studies. Beyond the classroom, Scholars participate in leadership development activities, high-level lectures from heads of state and business leaders, and cultural immersion experiences across China. The Schwarzman Scholars alumni network spans government, business, civil society, and media across 100+ countries — the kind of network that Nigerians building for Africa genuinely need.

The US/Global application opened on 8 April 2026 and closes hard at 3:00 p.m. EDT on 9 September 2026 — late submissions are not accepted under any circumstances. The application is entirely online and requires essays, a CV, three letters of recommendation, and academic transcripts. There is no application fee. Start at least 6–8 weeks before the deadline to give your essays and recommenders enough preparation time.

Deadlines and details can change — always confirm on the official page before applying. Keen Africa never charges for information about opportunities, and neither should anyone else.
