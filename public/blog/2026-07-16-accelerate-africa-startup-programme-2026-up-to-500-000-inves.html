Accelerate Africa Startup Programme 2026 (Up to $500,000 Investment for Early-Stage African Founders)

Deadline 25 July 2026
Eligibility Early-stage African founders building globally impactful businesses; open to all African nationalities including Nigerians
Apply now →

The Accelerate Africa Startup Programme 2026 is a pan-African early-stage accelerator designed for founders who are building businesses with global ambitions. Selected startups receive structured, expert-led guidance on product-market fit, business model refinement, fundraising readiness, and growth strategy — alongside direct access to the Future Africa investment fund. The most promising ventures from the cohort can receive investment of between $250,000 and $500,000, making this one of the most capital-heavy early-stage programmes available to African founders today.

The programme is specifically designed for founders who are past the idea stage but need the strategic scaffolding to scale confidently. Mentors are drawn from the African and global startup ecosystem, with deep expertise in fintech, healthtech, agtech, climate, and consumer technology. The accelerator also provides ongoing access to an alumni network of founders across the continent.

Nigerian startups are among the most competitive applicants to Accelerate Africa. The application deadline is July 25, 2026 — with a simple online submission process. If you have early traction, a founding team, and a clear problem you are solving for a large market, this is one of the most credible non-dilutive-friendly accelerator paths available to you right now.

Deadlines and details can change — always confirm on the official page before applying. Keen Africa never charges for information about opportunities, and neither should anyone else.
