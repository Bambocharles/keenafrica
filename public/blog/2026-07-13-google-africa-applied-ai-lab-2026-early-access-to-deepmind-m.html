Google Africa Applied AI Lab 2026: Early Access to DeepMind Models + Funding

Deadline 31 August 2026
Eligibility African AI startup founders and researchers building technology solutions for African challenges across any sector; open continent-wide.
Apply now →

The Google Africa Applied AI Lab is a landmark initiative established by Google Labs through the Accra AI Community Centre (AICC) in Ghana, backed by the Google AI Futures Fund. Applications opened on 1 July 2026 and close on 31 August 2026. Unlike a typical accelerator, this program gives participants exclusive early access to cutting-edge Google DeepMind AI models before public release, a significant technical advantage for building products that compete globally.

Selected participants receive personalized technical mentorship from Google engineers, commercialization strategy support, direct connections to venture capital, and may become eligible for investment through the Google AI Futures Fund. The program targets founders and researchers building AI for education, healthcare, finance, creative industries, agriculture, or any other sector facing uniquely African challenges. It is designed to help produce Africa’s first generation of AI-native unicorn companies.

The application is submitted through Google’s official portal. Prepare a strong articulation of your AI product idea, the African problem it solves, and your technical background. Selected applicants will be notified in September 2026. This opportunity will not repeat in the same form; it is early-stage, intentionally small, and extremely high-value. Apply as early as possible.
