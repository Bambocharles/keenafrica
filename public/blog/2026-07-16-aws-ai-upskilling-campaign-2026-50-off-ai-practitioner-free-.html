AWS and Pearson VUE are running a limited-time AI upskilling campaign that makes two globally recognised cloud certifications significantly more affordable. That is two globally recognised AWS certifications for the cost of half of one.

The AWS Certified AI Practitioner is designed for non-technical and tech-adjacent professionals. It covers AI, machine learning, and generative AI concepts, AWS AI services, and practical applications — no coding background required. The Cloud Practitioner is the foundational AWS credential valued by employers across Africa and globally. Both certifications are taken online through Pearson VUE, meaning you can sit the exam from anywhere in Nigeria.
