3MTT (3 Million Technical Talent) is the Nigerian Federal Government’s flagship digital skills initiative, run through the Ministry of Communications, Innovation and Digital Economy and NITDA. The DeepTech_Ready track sits above the general 3MTT cohorts, focused specifically on Data Science, AI, and other advanced technical fields, built in partnership with Data Science Nigeria and supported by Google.org.

There is no application fee, no IELTS requirement, and no fixed closing date, applications are accepted continuously as new cohorts form. Each cohort runs for six months of structured technical and professional development, ending in certification.

The programme has already trained over 135,000 fellows directly since its 2023 launch, and in 2026 it opened a Talent Registry connecting graduates to more than 3,000 active tech job listings. For a Nigerian who wants advanced AI or data science skills without paying for a bootcamp, this is the most direct government-backed route available.
