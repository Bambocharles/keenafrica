MEST AI Startup Program (Class of 2027): Fully Sponsored AI Entrepreneur Fellowship

Deadline 20 July 2026
Eligibility West and East African citizens aged 21–35 with software development experience, basic AI understanding, and entrepreneurial drive; Nigerians eligible with in-person interviews held in Nigeria.
Apply now →

MEST (Meltwater Entrepreneurial School of Technology) is one of Africa’s longest-running and most respected startup incubators. The Class of 2027 is open now, with applications closing 20 July 2026. This is a fully-sponsored, full-time residential program, no tuition, no fees, and selected participants have their flights to Accra covered. The program runs 7 months of hands-on AI training, followed by a 4-month incubation phase for the most promising ventures, with top teams pitching for up to $100,000 in pre-seed investment.

The curriculum is co-delivered with practitioners from Google, Meta, OpenAI, and leading African enterprises. You will form a team, validate a real AI business idea, build and deploy a minimum viable product, and pitch to investors at Demo Day. The focus for the Class of 2027 is West and East Africa, and in-person interviews are explicitly being held in Nigeria (as well as Ghana, Senegal, and Kenya), making this directly accessible to Nigerians without expensive travel.

To apply, you must complete an online application form plus a required online assessment, both must be submitted before the deadline. Applications are reviewed on a rolling basis, so applying early gives you a faster review. If you are a developer who has always wanted to build an AI company with real structure, mentorship, and funding behind it, MEST is the most serious launchpad on the continent.

Deadlines and details can change; always confirm on the official page before applying. Keen Africa never charges for information about opportunities, and neither should anyone else.
