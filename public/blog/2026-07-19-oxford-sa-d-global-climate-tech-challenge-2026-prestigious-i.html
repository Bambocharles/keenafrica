Oxford Saïd Global Climate Tech Challenge 2026 — Prestigious Innovation Competition

Deadline 17 August 2026
Eligibility Open to entrepreneurs, innovators, students, and organisations globally working on scalable climate technology solutions; teams or individuals welcome.
Apply now →

The Oxford Saïd Global Climate Tech Challenge is one of the most prestigious innovation competitions focused on climate technology, backed by Oxford Saïd Business School — one of the world’s leading business schools. The 2026 Challenge is open for applications until August 17, 2026, and invites entrepreneurs, innovators, and organisations from around the world to submit scalable, technology-driven solutions addressing the global climate crisis. African entrants building solutions relevant to the continent’s energy, agriculture, water, or infrastructure challenges are particularly well-positioned.

Finalists gain access to Oxford Saïd’s global network of investors, faculty, and alumni, as well as structured mentorship, media exposure, and the credibility of being associated with one of the world’s most respected academic institutions. For a Nigerian founder or researcher working on climate-relevant technology, placing as a finalist or winner in this competition is a career-defining signal — opening doors to international investors, grant programmes, and partnership opportunities that would otherwise take years to access.

The application involves pitching your solution, demonstrating its scalability and impact potential, and articulating your team’s capability to execute. If you are building anything in clean energy, sustainable agriculture, water technology, or climate adaptation in Nigeria, apply now. The August 17 deadline gives you less than a month from today — move fast and build a compelling submission.
