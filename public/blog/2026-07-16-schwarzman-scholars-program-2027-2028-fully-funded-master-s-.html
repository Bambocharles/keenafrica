Schwarzman Scholars Program 2027–2028 (Fully Funded Master's at Tsinghua University, China)

Deadline 9 September 2026
Eligibility Ages 18–28 with a completed or in-progress undergraduate degree; open to all nationalities worldwide including Nigerians
Apply now →

The Schwarzman Scholars Program is one of the most prestigious fully funded graduate fellowships in the world. It covers 100% of tuition, accommodation, international flights, health insurance, and provides a monthly stipend — all in exchange for one year of intensive study and leadership development at Tsinghua University in Beijing. The curriculum blends global affairs, economics, public policy, and leadership practicums, with direct exposure to world-class thinkers, policymakers, and business leaders.

The programme is run by Schwarzman Scholars, a US-based nonprofit, and selects roughly 150 scholars per year from across the globe. Selection is based on academic excellence, extraordinary leadership potential, entrepreneurial spirit, and cross-cultural empathy — not just grades. Nigerian applicants have no disadvantage here; the programme actively seeks diversity from underrepresented regions.

The application is entirely online, requires two essays, short-answer responses, three letters of recommendation, and an official transcript. Shortlisted candidates are invited to in-person interviews in London or New York in October/November 2026. Start your application early — the deadline is firm at 3:00 p.m. EDT on 9 September 2026, with no exceptions for technical issues. This is the kind of programme that reshapes careers and networks for decades.
