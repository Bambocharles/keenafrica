Google Career Certificates via Coursera Financial Aid

Deadline Rolling
Eligibility Any learner globally who demonstrates financial need, including Nigerian applicants
Apply now →

Google Career Certificates are designed to make someone job-ready in three to six months without a prior degree, covering some of the most in-demand entry points into tech: IT Support, Data Analytics, Cybersecurity, UX Design, Digital Marketing, and Project Management. Normally hosted on Coursera at roughly $39 a month, the certificates become free for learners who qualify for financial aid.

The application is built into the Coursera enrolment flow: on any Google Career Certificate course page, click Financial Aid Available next to the Enroll button and complete a short form explaining your financial situation and career goals. There is no separate deadline or cohort structure, it runs continuously.

Google also distributes certificate scholarships through partner organisations active in Nigeria, so learners who do not qualify directly through Coursera’s financial aid form have a second route through local NGO and community partners. Either way, the certificates themselves carry real weight with employers who already recognise the Google name.

Deadlines and details can change; always confirm on the official page before applying. Keen Africa never charges for information about opportunities, and neither should anyone else.
