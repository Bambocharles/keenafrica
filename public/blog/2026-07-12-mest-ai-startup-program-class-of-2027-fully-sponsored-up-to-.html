MEST AI Startup Program — Class of 2027 (Fully Sponsored, Up to $100,000 Pre-Seed)

Deadline 20 July 2026
Eligibility West and East African nationals aged 21–35 with software development experience and entrepreneurial interest; interviews held in Ghana, Nigeria, Senegal, and Kenya; full-time commitment for up to 12 months required.
Apply now →

The MEST AI Startup Program (Class of 2027) is MEST Africa’s flagship initiative, now in its second year with a sharpened focus on artificial intelligence. The programme is fully sponsored — you pay nothing to MEST — and covers a 7-month intensive in-person training phase in Accra, Ghana, plus a potential additional 4-month incubation phase for the strongest emerging ventures. Selected participants receive flights to Ghana, healthcare access during the programme, world-class curriculum from practitioners at Google, Meta, and OpenAI, and direct access to MEST’s portfolio fund for pre-seed investment of up to $100,000 for ventures that make it through incubation.

The 7-month training is structured to take you from idea exploration through team formation, MVP development, and deployment — all while working with real customers. You will be embedded in a pan-African cohort of peers building AI startups across sectors from fintech to agtech to health. For the 2027 intake, MEST is recruiting specifically from West and East Africa, with in-person interviews and selection challenges held in Ghana, Nigeria, Senegal, and Kenya — meaning Lagos and Akure-based applicants can compete locally.

Applications opened on 19 May 2026 and close on 20 July 2026. The application is reviewed on a rolling basis, so early submission gets earlier review. You will need to complete an online form and a required assessment; incomplete applications are not reviewed. This is one of the most structured, resource-rich pathways for Nigerian AI founders to go from idea to investment-ready company inside a single year — with zero tuition cost.

Deadlines and details can change — always confirm on the official page before applying. Keen Africa never charges for information about opportunities, and neither should anyone else.
