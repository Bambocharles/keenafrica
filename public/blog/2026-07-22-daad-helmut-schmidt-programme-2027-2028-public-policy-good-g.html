DAAD Helmut-Schmidt Programme 2027/2028 — Public Policy & Good Governance

Deadline 31 July 2026
Eligibility Nigerian graduates with a bachelor's degree in social/political sciences, law, economics, or related fields; relevant professional or civic experience required
Apply now →

The DAAD Helmut-Schmidt Programme, funded by Germany’s Federal Foreign Office, supports emerging leaders from developing and emerging countries to pursue Master’s degrees in disciplines critical to governance and development — political science, public policy, law, economics, and administration. Nigeria is explicitly eligible, and this window (1 June – 31 July 2026) is the active application period for the 2027/2028 academic year.

The funding package includes: exemption from tuition fees at German public universities, a monthly stipend of approximately €992, health insurance contributions, travel allowance, study and research subsidy, rent subsidies where applicable, and a 4-month German language course prior to the Master’s programme. The scholarship duration covers the full standard duration of the degree, typically 2 years.

Applicants apply directly through the DAAD portal and simultaneously to their chosen German Master’s programme. The 2023 DAAD Annual Report shows Nigeria ranks among the top 7 countries globally in DAAD scholarship reception — 1,638 Nigerians received DAAD funding that year alone. If you are working in policy, governance, civil society, or law, this is one of the strongest fully funded pathways available to you.
