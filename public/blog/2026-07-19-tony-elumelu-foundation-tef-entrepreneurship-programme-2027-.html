Tony Elumelu Foundation (TEF) Entrepreneurship Programme 2027 — $5,000 Seed Capital + Training

Deadline 1 March 2027 (applications open 1 January 2027)
Eligibility African entrepreneurs from all 54 African countries with a business idea or early-stage business; open to all sectors; no age cap, but prioritises young founders.
Apply now →

The Tony Elumelu Foundation Entrepreneurship Programme is the flagship pan-African initiative that has, since 2015, funded over 24,000 entrepreneurs, trained 2.5 million Africans, created more than 1.5 million jobs, and generated over $4.2 billion in revenue. Each selected entrepreneur receives $5,000 in non-refundable seed capital (not a loan, not equity), intensive business training delivered through TEFConnect’s digital platform, one-on-one expert mentorship, and direct access to Africa’s largest entrepreneurship network, which connects founders with investors, partners, and peers across all 54 African countries.

The 2026 cycle closed in March 2026, but the 2027 cycle will open on January 1, 2027, and run until March 1, 2027. That gives you over five months to prepare your application, sharpen your business narrative, and position yourself competitively. The selection process evaluates business viability, scalability, social impact, and the founder’s clarity of vision — not just the idea itself. Women now make up 46% of TEF entrepreneurs, the highest female participation rate recorded by a programme of this scale.

If you missed 2026, the right move is to start preparing now. Write your business plan, refine your value proposition, and document any early traction you have built. TEFConnect is the platform — create your profile and keep it updated so you are ready to submit the moment the portal opens in January. This is the most accessible and impactful first-cheque programme on the continent.

Deadlines and details can change — always confirm on the official page before applying. Keen Africa never charges for information about opportunities, and neither should anyone else.
