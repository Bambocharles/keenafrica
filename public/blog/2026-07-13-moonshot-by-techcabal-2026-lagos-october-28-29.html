Moonshot by TechCabal 2026: Lagos, October 28–29

Moonshot by TechCabal is one of the most culturally significant startup events in Nigeria, not just a conference but a festival that combines hard-nosed investor conversations with the energy and ambition of Lagos’s tech ecosystem. The 2026 edition runs on October 28–29 at the Eko Convention Centre in Lagos, making it the most accessible high-quality conference on this list for Nigerians who cannot travel internationally.

The event brings together hundreds of founders, venture capitalists, corporate decision-makers, and ecosystem operators for panels, workshops, live demos, and networking sessions. TechCabal, the organiser, is the most respected independent tech journalism platform in Africa, which means the attendee quality is consistently high: these are serious people making real deals. Speakers and panelists are drawn from the most consequential African tech companies and investment firms.

Early-bird registration typically opens several months before the event and offers the most affordable entry point. Startup exhibitor and speaker applications are also accepted, for founders with a pitch-ready product, a spotlight at Moonshot can generate investor conversations that take months to otherwise arrange. Register early, prepare your deck, and show up ready to be in the room.
