IFC SME Growth Accelerator 2026 — Up to $1.5 Million for African SMEs

Deadline 15 November 2026
Eligibility For-profit SMEs with 10–250 employees and verified annual revenue between $250,000 and $10 million, operational in Africa (among other eligible regions); no application fee.
Apply now →

The IFC SME Growth Accelerator is a grant programme from the International Finance Corporation — the private sector arm of the World Bank Group — targeting high-growth small and medium enterprises in emerging markets. Successful applicants can receive between $50,000 and $1.5 million in grant funding, applicable to working capital, technology and equipment, market research, capacity building, and sustainability initiatives. The programme also provides mentorship, access to IFC’s global business networks, and opportunities for follow-on financing — a package that goes significantly beyond a one-time cheque.

Eligibility is clear: your business must be a registered for-profit entity with 10 to 250 employees and annual revenue verified between $250,000 and $10 million, operating in Africa, Asia, or Latin America. This makes it most relevant to Nigerian businesses that have passed the startup phase and are ready to scale operationally or enter new markets. Applications are reviewed against World Bank Group standards, and the process is designed to be accessible — submissions take approximately 15 minutes, there are no application fees, and decisions typically come within 4 to 6 weeks.

The deadline is 15 November 2026, which gives serious founders adequate time to prepare a strong submission. Apply as early as possible since rolling review means earlier applications get faster decisions. If your business has traction, a clear growth plan, and verifiable financials, this is one of the most credible large-scale non-dilutive grant opportunities currently open in Africa. Gather your revenue documents, employee records, and growth strategy before you open the form.
