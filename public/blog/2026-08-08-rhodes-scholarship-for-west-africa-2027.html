Rhodes Scholarship for West Africa 2027

Deadline 27 August 2026
Eligibility Citizens of West African countries, including Nigeria, aged 18 to 27, with outstanding academic and leadership record
Apply now →

The Rhodes Scholarship is the oldest and among the most prestigious postgraduate awards in the world, funding a degree of your choice at the University of Oxford. It covers Oxford course fees in full, an annual stipend of £20,400, the Oxford application fee, visa and healthcare surcharge costs, two economy flights, and a settling-in allowance. The West Africa constituency, which includes Nigeria, selects a small cohort each year through a rigorous, no-fee process.

Rhodes Trust looks beyond grades: it wants evidence of energetic use of your talents, moral force of character, and instincts to lead and serve others. Past West African Scholars have gone on to run development banks, build unicorn startups, and shape national policy, which is the kind of trajectory the selection committee is scouting for.

The 2027 cycle opened 1 June 2026 and closes 27 August 2026, so this is a tight window. Applicants need a strong academic transcript, references that speak to character rather than just competence, and a personal statement that reads like a mission, not a resume.

Deadlines and details can change; always confirm on the official page before applying. Keen Africa never charges for information about opportunities, and neither should anyone else.
