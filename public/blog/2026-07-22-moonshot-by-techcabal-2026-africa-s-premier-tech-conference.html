Moonshot by TechCabal 2026: Africa's Premier Tech Conference

Deadline 28 October 2026
Eligibility Open to all, founders, investors, developers, students, policymakers, and tech enthusiasts; TC Battlefield pitch competition open to early-stage African startups
Apply now →

Moonshot by TechCabal has emerged as Africa’s fastest-growing technology conference and most consequential annual convening of the continent’s tech ecosystem. The 2025 edition welcomed 6,000 attendees from 39 countries across 6 continents. The 2026 edition, themed ‘Courage & Conviction, Building for a New World’, returns to the National Theatre in Lagos on October 28–29, 2026, with investors attending who have collectively deployed over $5 billion through their funds.

The conference runs nine content tracks, Future of Commerce, Creative Economy, Emerging Tech (AI & ML), Government & Policy, Startup Festival, FUEL: The Investors Conference, Clean Tech & Climate, Big Tech & Enterprise, and Entering Tech, plus a full week of co-located side events, mixers, and the TC Battlefield startup pitch competition where finalists compete for a prize pool historically split across $15,000, $10,000, and $5,000 prizes. Applications to speak at the conference are currently open.

Tickets are available for purchase now at the official site. For builders in Akure and across Nigeria, Moonshot is the single most valuable room you can put yourself in for investor access, partnership deals, and direct exposure to the decision-makers shaping Africa’s tech future. TC Battlefield applications are free, apply to pitch your startup on the continent’s biggest stage.
