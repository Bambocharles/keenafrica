One Young World Leading Scholarship 2026

Deadline 31 October 2026
Eligibility Young leaders aged 18-35 with a demonstrated record of impact, priority given to first-time Summit attendees from under-represented countries
Apply now →

One Young World is the closest thing the youth leadership world has to a global mainstage: 2,200 delegates from 190-plus countries, and a lifelong Ambassador network of over 20,000 alumni that includes people who go on to run companies, NGOs, and government initiatives. The 2026 Summit takes place in Cape Town from 3 to 6 November, which puts it within easy reach of Nigerian delegates in terms of both cost and time zone.

The Leading Scholarship covers round-trip travel, hotel accommodation for the full Summit, and all meals, so the only real cost to a successful applicant is time. Selection prioritises candidates who can point to specific, verifiable impact in their community or field, not just potential.

Beyond the four days on stage, delegates get access to the Action Accelerator programme, mentorship from global leaders, and in many cases the opening to represent Nigeria during the Summit’s Opening Ceremony. It is one of the few free routes onto a genuinely international stage before the age of 35.

Deadlines and details can change; always confirm on the official page before applying. Keen Africa never charges for information about opportunities, and neither should anyone else.
