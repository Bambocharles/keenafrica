GREAT Scholarships 2026–27 for Nigerian Students (UK Universities)

Deadline Rolling — varies by university (check individual institution pages)
Eligibility Nigerian nationals applying for a one-year taught postgraduate programme at a participating UK university for the 2026–27 academic year; self-funded applicants only.
Apply now →

GREAT Scholarships are a partnership between the British Council and the UK government’s GREAT Britain Campaign, designed to give high-achieving international students meaningful financial support to study in the United Kingdom. For the 2026–27 academic year, Nigerian students can access £10,000 toward tuition fees at participating institutions including Brunel University London, Cranfield University, Imperial College London, UCL, University of Warwick, University of Bath, University of Kent, University of Southampton, Royal Holloway, Nottingham Trent University, and Edge Hill University — among others.

Each university sets its own eligibility criteria, available subject areas, and application deadline, so the British Council’s Nigeria page serves as the central directory. The award is not means-tested — it is merit-based, awarded to applicants who meet the academic and personal profile requirements of the individual university. In return, successful scholars agree to attend a GREAT Scholars networking event and act as ambassadors for studying in the UK, which itself can be a significant networking and visibility opportunity.

To apply, you must first apply for and receive a conditional or unconditional offer from the university’s postgraduate programme, then submit the scholarship application directly to that university. Application timelines are set at the university level, so check the British Council Nigeria page immediately to see which institutions still have open windows. Self-funded Nigerian students who already have UK university offers should prioritise this — £10,000 is a meaningful contribution to first-year costs.

Deadlines and details can change — always confirm on the official page before applying. Keen Africa never charges for information about opportunities, and neither should anyone else.
