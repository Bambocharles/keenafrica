### DAAD Scholarships — Development-Related Postgraduate Courses (Germany)
**Deadline:** Varies by programme; most October–January windows · **Eligibility:** Nigerian citizens holding a Bachelor's degree (minimum second-class upper preferred), typically with up to six years since undergraduate completion · **Apply:** [https://www.daad.de/en/study-and-research-in-germany/scholarships/](https://www.daad.de/en/study-and-research-in-germany/scholarships/)


The DAAD (Deutscher Akademischer Austauschdienst) — the German Academic Exchange Service — is one of the world's largest scholarship organisations. It offers numerous funding programmes for Nigerian students pursuing Master's degrees and PhDs at German universities, where tuition at public universities is free or nominal even without a scholarship.
 
The monthly stipend is €992 for Master's students (€1,400 for doctoral candidates as of 2026), plus health, accident, and personal liability insurance, as well as a travel subsidy for flights from Nigeria to Germany.
 
The main programmes relevant to Nigerian students include the Development-Related Postgraduate Courses (EPOS), the In-Country/In-Region Programme, and the Leadership for Africa scholarship.
 Germany is one of the most underutilised destinations among Nigerian applicants — tuition at public universities is near zero even without the DAAD grant, making this a double win.
