Scholarships

Chevening Scholarship 2027/2028 (UK Government)

Deadline: 6 October 2026 · Eligibility: Nigerian citizens with an undergraduate degree (minimum 2:1 equivalent), at least two years of work experience, and commitment to return to Nigeria after studies · Apply: https://www.chevening.org/scholarship/nigeria/

The UK Government’s Chevening Scholarships and Fellowships programme has opened applications for the 2026/2027 academic year, allowing globally talented individuals to apply for fully funded study and professional development opportunities in the UK, from 5 August to 7 October 2026.

What Chevening covers: complete tuition fees for any eligible Master’s programme at any UK university; a monthly living allowance of approximately £1,347; economy return airfare to the UK; an arrival allowance; a departure allowance; visa application costs. This is the gold standard of postgraduate funding. Nigeria is one of the largest recipients of Chevening Scholarships in the world — Nigerian doctors, lawyers, engineers, journalists, public servants, and entrepreneurs have all won Chevening and returned home with not just a UK master’s degree, but an international network that opens doors throughout their careers. The application window opens in August 2026 — begin drafting your essays and securing referees now.

MEST AI Startup Program — Class of 2027 (Meltwater Foundation, Fully Sponsored)

Deadline: 20 July 2026 · Eligibility: West or East African citizens aged 21–35 with software development experience, basic AI understanding, and entrepreneurial interest; full-time commitment of up to 12 months required · Apply: https://mestaistartupprogram.smapply.us/prog/mest_ai_startup_program_class_of_2027/

Applications are open from 19 May 2026 and close 20 July 2026, with applications reviewed on a rolling basis.

This fully-sponsored program brings selected participants to Accra, Ghana for an intensive residential experience focused on training, team formation, product development, and venture incubation. The program runs for up to 12 months — fellows complete a 7-month in-person training phase, then selected ventures continue into a 4-month incubation phase.

Over 7 months, you will form a team, validate a real AI business idea, build and deploy an MVP, and learn directly from world-class practitioners at Google, Meta, OpenAI, and beyond.

Fellows receive a fully-sponsored program with no financial payments to MEST, a flight to Ghana covered for admitted participants, healthcare access during the program, and the opportunity to receive up to $100,000 in pre-seed investment for high-performing ventures. Nigerians are explicitly in the interview cohort — interviews are held in-person in Nigeria.

DAAD Scholarships — Development-Related Postgraduate Courses (Germany)

Deadline: Varies by programme; most October–January windows · Eligibility: Nigerian citizens holding a Bachelor’s degree (minimum second-class upper preferred), typically with up to six years since undergraduate completion · Apply: https://www.daad.de/en/study-and-research-in-germany/scholarships/

The DAAD (Deutscher Akademischer Austauschdienst) — the German Academic Exchange Service — is one of the world’s largest scholarship organisations. It offers numerous funding programmes for Nigerian students pursuing Master’s degrees and PhDs at German universities, where tuition at public universities is free or nominal even without a scholarship.

The monthly stipend is €992 for Master’s students (€1,400 for doctoral candidates as of 2026), plus health, accident, and personal liability insurance, as well as a travel subsidy for flights from Nigeria to Germany.

The main programmes relevant to Nigerian students include the Development-Related Postgraduate Courses (EPOS), the In-Country/In-Region Programme, and the Leadership for Africa scholarship. Germany is one of the most underutilised destinations among Nigerian applicants — tuition at public universities is near zero even without the DAAD grant, making this a double win.

Accelerate Africa Startup Programme 2026 (Future Africa)

Deadline: 25 July 2026 · Eligibility: Early-stage African startup founders building technology-driven, globally scalable ventures; at least two co-founders recommended; no application fee, no upfront equity · Apply: https://acceler8.africa/

Early-stage startup founders across Africa building high-growth, globally ambitious ventures are invited to apply for the Accelerate Africa Startup Programme 2026 — an initiative pioneered by Future Africa in collaboration with a network of co-investors, ecosystem partners, and industry experts. The programme supports bold founders tackling Africa’s most pressing challenges through technology-driven, innovation-led businesses with the potential to scale globally.

There is no application fee and no upfront equity required to apply. Accelerate Africa is designed to give early-stage founders the hands-on support, expert guidance, and investor access needed to build globally competitive businesses rooted in Africa. Beyond the programme itself, selected startups gain direct access to the Future Africa fund and potential investment of between USD $250,000 and $500,000.

The Accelerate Africa Startup Program is an 8-week accelerator focused on supporting bold African founders solving the continent’s biggest challenges through innovation.

ECOWAS Regional Business Incubation Programme (EBIP) 2026

Deadline: 16 July 2026 · Eligibility: Young people aged 18–35 who are nationals or residents of Benin, Côte d’Ivoire, The Gambia, Ghana, Guinea-Bissau, Senegal, or Sierra Leone, with an innovative business idea in the pre-launch stage · Apply: https://eywep.cdjscedeao.org/

⚠️ Note: The current 2026 pilot phase of EBIP targets seven ECOWAS countries — Nigeria is not yet included. However, this is a West African regional programme and Nigerians in diaspora residing in these countries, or those monitoring for future expansion cycles, should track this programme closely.

Applications are now open for the 2026 ECOWAS Regional Business Incubation Programme, an initiative under the ECOWAS Youth and Women’s Entrepreneurship Programme (EYWEP). Selected participants will receive structured business incubation, entrepreneurship training, project maturation workshops, one-on-one mentoring and coaching, business model and business plan development, support for legal business registration, and networking opportunities with technical, financial, and institutional partners.

Prizes include $15,000 for 1st place, $10,000 for 2nd place, $5,000 for 3rd place, and a $5,000 special award for Best Regional Business Plan.

Participation in the program is free of charge.

Milken-Motsepe Prize in Circular Economy 2026 ($2 Million)

Deadline: 13 August 2026 · Eligibility: Companies with over 2 years of continuous operation on the African continent, more than US$500,000 in revenue and raised capital, existing institutional or corporate partnerships, and solutions currently deployed in Africa · Apply: https://milkenmotsepeprize.org/circular-economy/

The Milken-Motsepe Prize in Circular Economy calls on scalable companies using technology to enhance circular economy practices across a variety of industries in Africa. This prize offers $2 million in total prizes, including a $1 million Grand Prize. Participating companies will also gain access to exclusive networking and the opportunity to pitch to industry leaders and key decision-makers.

Ten semi-finalists will receive $50,000 each; five finalists will receive $50,000 each; one company will receive the $1 million Grand Prize and one company will receive a $250,000 Runner-Up Prize.

There is no cost to register or to participate. If your startup is in waste tech, agri-processing, fashion, construction, electronics, or food systems — and you have traction — this is one of the biggest innovation prizes on the continent right now.

Free Training & Certifications

AWS Skill Builder — 600+ Free Cloud & AI Courses (Amazon Web Services)

Deadline: Rolling/Always Open · Eligibility: Anyone globally, any age · Enrol: https://skillbuilder.aws/

AWS Skill Builder is an online learning center where you can learn from AWS experts and build cloud skills online. With access to 600+ free courses, certification exam prep, and training that allows you to build practical skills, there’s something for everyone.

AWS Builder Labs offer FREE hands-on labs in real AWS environments (previously paid content, now free) — do these after completing foundational courses to solidify your understanding. Start with the AWS Cloud Practitioner Essentials course, earn a digital badge, and work your way toward the AWS Solutions Architect Associate — one of the most in-demand cloud credentials globally. In Kenya and Nigeria, cloud engineers with AWS Associate or Professional credentials command senior salaries between KES 250,000–900,000 monthly per Techpression’s 2026 Africa tech salary analysis. No payment card required to access the free tier.

Microsoft Learn — Free Azure Training Paths (Microsoft)

Deadline: Rolling/Always Open · Eligibility: Anyone globally · Enrol: https://learn.microsoft.com/

Microsoft offers over 3,600 online courses and tutorials with 3,200+ currently free. Microsoft’s courses are primarily available through Microsoft Learn, an interactive, hands-on platform that offers free access to learning paths and modules.

The Azure Fundamentals path on Microsoft Learn is free, covers Azure cloud concepts, core services, security, compliance, pricing, and support, and prepares you for the Azure Fundamentals (AZ-900) certification exam. Microsoft provides comprehensive free training for their cloud platform, including hands-on labs.

Learning paths on Microsoft Learn include free digital badges you can share directly on LinkedIn. The AZ-900 exam itself requires payment, but the training to pass it is completely free.

Google Cloud Skills Boost — Free Labs & Badges (Google Cloud)

Deadline: Rolling/Always Open; Google Cloud Arcade challenges run monthly · Eligibility: Anyone globally · Enrol: https://cloudskillsboost.google/

Google Cloud Skills Boost provides hands-on labs (Qwiklabs) where you work in real GCP environments — start with the “Getting Started with Google Cloud” learning path, which is free and takes approximately 8 hours. Google Cloud Arcade is a gamified learning platform with monthly challenges that keep you engaged while earning badges and prizes.

Complete the full course and you’ll be awarded an industry-recognized badge that you can share on your personal and professional profiles. The Arcade monthly challenges are particularly powerful for Nigerians — complete enough challenges and you earn free Google swag, cloud credits, and stackable credentials. No credit card required for the free-tier labs.
